### 8. Box CEO Aaron Levie — AI 生产力上限两年内从 30–50% 跳到 5–10 倍，前沿与掉队的差距已达数量级

**EN:** Box CEO Aaron Levie argues the productivity gap between AI power users and everyone else is at an all-time high precisely because adoption is at its broadest (391 likes). A few years ago, even in coding — the most capability-aligned field — the best you could get was "maybe 30-50% better productivity," with time lost to "cleaning up the mistakes of models," limited context windows, and "highly synchronous interactions." Now agents run far longer tasks "with limited human interaction," models are trained on "vastly better datasets for a growing array of domains so you have to intervene far less," and systems "better support working with many agents at once." His math: "the upper limits on productivity gains have gone from 30-50% to now likely 5-10X depending on the field" — meaning "living at the frontier vs. not paying attention at all could be an order of magnitude difference in your output," a gap he expects to keep widening "given the rate of progress we're likely to see with RSI."

**中文：** Box CEO Aaron Levie 提出一个略带悖论的判断：AI 产出差距之大正处于历史之最——恰恰因为 AI 采用广度也正处于历史之最（391 赞）。几年前，即使编码这种能力最对齐的领域，顶尖用户也就「提升 30% 到 50% 的生产力」，还要花不少时间「收拾模型的错误」，上下文窗口太短，交互高度同步。而现在，agent 能「在极少人工干预下」执行长任务，模型在越来越多领域「用好得多的数据集训练，需要介入的次数少得多」，系统也「更能支撑同时与多个 agent 协作」。他的算术：「生产力增益的上限在短短两年内从 30–50% 走到了大概 5–10 倍（视领域而定）」——意味着「活在前沿和完全不在意之间，产出可能相差一个数量级」，而且他判断这个差距还会随 RSI 的进步速度继续拉大。

**为什么值得看：** 这不是「AI 有用」的泛泛之谈，而是一个可检验的量化断言：30–50% 与 5–10 倍。对任何组织，这意味着同一行业内的竞争者正在被拉开数量级差距——也解释了为什么企业宁可忍受 Masad 说的「混乱期」也要上 agent。

The operational details are unusually concrete. On the NVIDIA relationship: prototypes a year ahead, then "we've optimized things.
