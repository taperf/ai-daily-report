**Peter Yang (AI tutorials & interviews)**

Yang shared his five biggest takeaways from Linear's leaders on building a production agent end-to-end: (1) Map the actual workflow first — identify where work starts, which systems hold context, and what actions complete it. (2) Start with a narrow, well-defined task. (3) Build for failure — agents will hallucinate, so design graceful fallbacks. (4) Measure quality, not just completion. (5) The hardest part is not the model, it's the orchestration and integration with existing systems. This is a practical synthesis from one of the teams most respected in the product-agent space.

Yang 分享了从 Linear 团队那里学到的关于端到端构建生产级 agent 的五大要点：(1) 先映射实际工作流——明确工作从哪里开始、哪些系统持有上下文、什么动作才算完成；(2) 从一个狭窄、定义明确的任务开始；(3) 为失败而设计——agent 会幻觉，所以要设计优雅的降级方案；(4) 衡量质量，而非仅仅是完成率；(5) 最难的部分不是模型，而是编排和与现有系统的集成。这是来自产品 agent 领域最受尊敬的团队之一的实用总结。

**Anthropic Engineering: How we contain Claude across products**

Anthropic published a detailed technical post on how they secure Claude across claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. The core argument: as model capabilities grow, the risk of autonomous agents taking harmful actions only increases — but the cost of not deploying them also grows. The engineering question becomes how to cap the blast radius.

Anthropic 发表了一篇详细的技术文章，介绍他们如何对 claude.ai、Claude Code 和 Claude Cowork 中的 Claude 进行安全隔离。核心论点：随着模型能力增长，自主 agent 采取有害行动的风险只会增加——但不部署它们的代价也在增长。工程问题就变成了如何限制爆炸半径。

They describe three containment patterns tailored to each product:
(1) **Ephemeral container** (claude.ai): Code runs in gVisor containers with no persistent filesystem — minimal blast radius, minimal ceiling.
(2) **Human-in-the-loop sandbox** (Claude Code): Reads allowed, writes sandboxed, network denied by default. An OS-level sandbox (Seatbelt on macOS, bubblewrap on Linux) reduced permission prompts by 84%.
(3) **Sealed VM** (Claude Cowork): Full virtual machine with hypervisor isolation, designed for non-technical users who can't judge bash commands.

他们描述了为每个产品量身定制的三种隔离模式：
(1) **临时容器**（claude.ai）：代码在 gVisor 容器中运行，无持久文件系统——爆炸半径最小，能力上限也最小。
(2) **人机协作沙盒**（Claude Code）：允许读取，写入沙盒化，默认禁止网络。操作系统级沙盒（macOS 上为 Seatbelt，Linux 上为 bubblewrap）将权限提示减少了 84%。
(3) **密封虚拟机**（Claude Cowork）：完整的虚拟机，使用 hypervisor 隔离，为无法判断 bash 命令的非技术用户设计。

Crucially, they disclosed several vulnerabilities they found: attackers could execute code through .claude/settings.json before the trust prompt appeared; a phishing prompt successfully exfiltrated AWS credentials 24 out of 25 times; and an approved-domain allowlist was exploited to exfiltrate data through Anthropic's own Files API. Their takeaway: deterministic environmental boundaries matter more than probabilistic model-layer defenses, because "the model layer couldn't help; there was nothing anomalous for it to catch."

最关键的是，他们披露了几处发现的漏洞：攻击者可以通过 .claude/settings.json 在信任提示出现之前执行代码；一个钓鱼 prompt 在 25 次尝试中成功窃取了 AWS 凭证 24 次；一个已批准域名的白名单被利用，通过 Anthropic 自己的 Files API 窃取了数据。他们的结论是：确定性的环境边界比概率性的模型层防御更重要，因为"模型层无能为力——没有任何异常供它捕捉。"

**No Priors: Building an Autonomous Enterprise for Real-World Services with Netic Founder Melisa Tokmak**

Melisa Tokmak, founder and CEO of Netic, discusses building AI agents for real-world service businesses — HVAC, plumbing, pet care, roofing, automotive, and wellness. Before Netic, she was a director of engineering at Scale AI and worked at Meta. She started Netic because she saw that most AI companies serve other tech companies, while the businesses that keep the world running — the ones her hometown in Turkey depends on — were being left behind.

Melisa Tokmak，Netic 创始人兼 CEO，讨论如何为现实世界的服务型企业构建 AI agent——涵盖 HVAC、水管、宠物护理、屋顶、汽车和 wellness。在创立 Netic 之前，她是 Scale AI 的工程总监，也曾在 Meta 工作。她创立 Netic 的动机是，她发现大多数 AI 公司都在服务其他科技公司，而那些让世界运转的企业——她土耳其家乡赖以生存的那些——却被抛在了后面。

**The Takeaway:** The real unlock for AI in essential services is not the model itself, but the "last mile" of harnesses, orchestration, and product built on top — understanding different accents, lifetime customer value, scheduling optimization, and multi-turn engagement across phone, text, and web. Over 70% of Netic's customers' first interaction with the company is now with a Netic agent, not a human.

**核心要点：** AI 在基础服务领域的真正突破不在于模型本身，而在于 harness、编排和产品层面的"最后一公里"——理解不同口音、客户终身价值、排程优化、以及跨电话、短信和网页的多轮互动。超过 70% 的 Netic 客户与公司的首次互动现在是和 Netic agent，而非人类。

Tokmak is skeptical of the "can the labs do this?" question, noting that the same question was asked about Google ten years ago. She argues that leading labs are great partners but not competitive risks for Netic, because they lack the focus and operational depth required for enterprise workflows. She also has a distinctive hiring philosophy: she screens for agency by asking candidates "what's the hardest thing you've ever done?" and digging for sustained commitment, not one-off achievements. She warns against the "AGI-pilled" mindset among Gen Z candidates who fear being "forever poor" if they don't make money in 18 months — a mindset she sees as dangerous for building anything that lasts.

Tokmak 对"实验室能不能做这个？"的问题持怀疑态度，指出十年前同样的问题被问到的是 Google。她认为领先实验室是很好的合作伙伴，但对 Netic 不构成竞争威胁，因为它们缺乏企业工作流所需的专注度和运营深度。她还有一套独特的招聘哲学：通过问候选人"你做过最难的事情是什么？"来筛选 agency，挖掘的是持续的承诺，而非一次性成就。她警告说，Gen Z 候选人中流行的"AGI-pilled"心态——担心如果 18 个月内赚不到钱就会"永远贫穷"——对构建任何能持久的东西来说是危险的。
