## 🎙️ 播客精选

**Training Data（Sequoia）| Box CEO Aaron Levie：AI 时代如何重塑自己与企业级扩散**

**The Takeaway:** The trillion-dollar question in AI isn't whether models get smarter — it's who bridges the vast gap between model capability and enterprise workflow, and Aaron Levie is betting that bridge is worth as much as the models themselves.

**The Takeaway：** AI 领域的万亿美元问题不在于模型会不会更聪明，而在于谁来填补模型能力与企业工作流之间的巨大鸿沟——Aaron Levie 赌的是，这座桥的价值不亚于模型本身。

**On the applied layer.** Levie's core thesis: "There's a massive chasm between the power of AI models and the ultimate workflows that enterprises are trying to to automate." Everyone in the Valley is "research pilled," assuming the model is the only thing that matters. But in the real world, workflows need data-system connections, human-in-the-loop moments, change management, and legacy-system integration. His historical analogy is blunt: ten years ago nobody would have predicted Snowflake or Databricks — "the infrastructure just already does that" — yet trillions in software value grew on top of AWS. The same will happen with intelligence. He estimates "a trillion dollars of applied layer AI value" is up for grabs.

**关于应用层。** Levie 的核心论点："AI 模型的能力与企业试图自动化的最终工作流之间存在巨大鸿沟。"硅谷所有人都被"研究思维"浸透，默认模型是唯一重要的事。但现实世界的工作流需要对接数据系统、需要人机协同的节点、需要变革管理、需要跟遗留系统整合。他的历史类比很直白：十年前没人能预言 Snowflake 和 Databricks 的存在——当时大家都觉得"基础设施不是已经能做到这些了吗"——结果软件层照样在 AWS 之上长出了数万亿美元的价值。智能领域也将如此。他估算"应用层 AI 有上万亿美元的价值"待攫取。

**On token economics.** The subsidization of tokens "just has to be a temporary phenomenon" — at tens of billions in capital, public-market capitalism eventually applies. But he doesn't see a winner-take-all outcome: Meta, SpaceX, China, even NVIDIA can happily run inference at 10% margins because they want compute utilization, which drives cost per token down on a like-for-like basis and shifts more value to the application layer. "The only thing I probably wouldn't bet on is one or two labs getting 95% of the value creation." And the labs shouldn't want that either: "At some point, you'll just be nationalized if you're the only thing that exists as intelligence."

**关于代币经济学。** 代币补贴"必然只是暂时现象"——当资本投入达到数百亿美元量级，资本主义的规律终将生效。但他不认为会出现赢家通吃：Meta、SpaceX、中国玩家甚至 NVIDIA 都可以接受 10% 的推理毛利，因为它们要的是算力利用率——这会推动同质代币成本持续下降，把更多价值推向应用层。"我唯一不会赌的，就是让一两家实验室拿走 95% 的价值创造。"而且实验室自己也不该想要这种结局："如果你是智能的唯一存在，某个时点你只会被国有化。"

**On work slop.** Why is AI-generated code embraced while AI-generated content triggers allergic reactions? Because for most of the world, code is a utility — if an agent generates your whole backend, that's preferable. But content is a proxy for a person's judgment: "I'm losing my ability to know how much of the thought process was them versus the AI." He predicts this weird collective negotiation takes 3-5 years to work through. His own reading habit has changed: "I'm now doing 2x the work to read these — once for the substance, and once for the calculation of, did the person write it, or am I just reading a Claude prompt?"

**关于 work slop（AI 垃圾工作产出）。** 为什么 AI 生成代码被拥抱、AI 生成内容却让人过敏？因为对大多数人来说，代码是工具——Agent 生成整个后端系统反而更好。但内容是个人判断力的代理指标："我正在失去判断思考过程有多少属于他本人、多少属于 AI 的能力。"他预测这场奇怪的社会集体博弈需要 3-5 年才能走完。他自己的阅读习惯已经改变："现在我读一篇 X 长文要花两倍功夫——一遍读实质内容，另一遍在计算：这是本人写的，还是我在读一个 Claude 提示词的产物？"

**On diffusion.** Coding agents spread instantly because code is 100% text, hyper-benchmarked, and its users are the most technical audience on earth. Knowledge work is the opposite: sales reps are rate-limited by external factors (does the customer even respond?), there's no "just give us your GitHub" onboarding for enterprise data trapped in legacy file shares, and access controls are radically harder. His warning: "Silicon Valley has to prepare for diffusion taking a lot longer than they think" — but that's precisely why the applied layer is worth a trillion dollars. And his boldest prediction: "In five years, 90% of all tokens in the enterprise are things a user never kicked off — they just see a result."

**关于 AI 扩散速度。** 编程 Agent 一夜普及，是因为代码 100% 是文本、被高频评测、而且用户是全球最具技术能力的人群。知识工作恰好相反：销售被外部因素限流（客户会不会回复你？）、企业数据困在遗留文件共享系统里没有"给我 GitHub 就行"式的接入、访问控制难度天差地别。他的警告："硅谷必须做好 AI 扩散比想象中慢得多的准备"——但这恰恰是应用层价值万亿的原因。他最大胆的预测："五年后，企业里 90% 的 token 消耗是用户从未主动发起的——他们只是看到结果。"

**Best quote:** "You're either a year ahead or a year behind simply based on your feed. Just join Twitter." — his emergency advice to every college sophomore.（"基于你的信息流，你要么领先一年，要么落后一年。赶紧上 Twitter。"——他给所有大二学生的紧急建议。）
