### No Priors: Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference（Inception 联合创始人兼 CEO Stefano Ermon）

**The Takeaway:** The next architecture battle in AI will not be won by training scale alone, but by whoever scales inference best, and Stefano Ermon believes diffusion models have a structural advantage there.

Ermon is a Stanford professor best known as one of the fathers of diffusion models; the score-based generative work from his 2019 lab became the foundation of modern image and video generation. Two years ago he founded Inception to bet the same technology can displace autoregressive models for text. His core argument is an economics one: autoregressive generation is inherently sequential, one token at a time, which makes inference memory-bound and a poor fit for GPUs. Diffusion generates many tokens in parallel, so its inference workload looks like training, which is exactly what GPUs are built for. "The bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win."

The evidence is no longer academic: Inception's Mercury models now match benchmark parity with speed-optimized frontier models while being significantly faster, are serving real production traffic, and convinced at least one voice-AI customer to switch away from custom inference silicon to plain NVIDIA GPUs. He also flagged an underappreciated property: diffusion models are easier to steer with external constraints mid-generation, and may be more data-efficient, which could matter as high-quality data becomes the bottleneck. His honest caveats: the ecosystem is immature (Inception had to build its own serving engine, training, and RL stack in house, which is why they chose not to open source), and nobody yet knows whether diffusion LLMs unlock capabilities autoregressive models cannot. On competing with giant labs, his moat is exactly that full-stack IP plus real customer feedback. Fun fact from his lab's history: FlashAttention and DPO both began as projects there.

**一句话结论：** AI 的下一场架构之战，胜负手不在训练规模，而在谁的推理扩展性最好，而 Stefano Ermon 认为扩散模型在这方面有结构性优势。

Ermon 是斯坦福教授、扩散模型之父之一，他实验室 2019 年的 score-based 生成模型工作，奠定了今天图像和视频生成的基础。两年前他创立 Inception，赌的是同一套技术也能在文本领域取代 autoregressive 模型。他的核心论点是经济学层面的：自回归生成天生是串行的、一次一个 token，导致推理严重受制于内存带宽、跟 GPU 格格不入；扩散模型并行生成大量 token，推理负载长得跟训练一样，恰恰是 GPU 最擅长的事。"The bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win."（残酷的教训是：更并行的方案终将胜出。）

证据已不止于学术层面：Inception 的 Mercury 模型在 benchmark 上追平了前沿实验室的速度优化模型，同时显著更快，已经在生产环境服务真实流量，还说服了一家语音 AI 客户从定制推理芯片切换到普通 NVIDIA GPU。他还点出两个被低估的特性：扩散模型可以在生成过程中用外部约束进行引导（可控性更强），以及可能更具数据效率，在高质量数据日益成为瓶颈的当下，这点会越来越重要。他也不回避短板：生态不成熟，Inception 只能自研 serving engine、训练和 RL 全套栈，这也是他们选择不开源的原因；至于扩散 LLM 能否解锁自回归模型做不到的能力，目前没人知道。面对大实验室的竞争，他的护城河恰恰是这套全栈 IP 加真实客户反馈。冷知识：FlashAttention 和 DPO 都源自他的实验室。
