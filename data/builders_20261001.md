### Strategy and market views

**Aaron Levie (Box CEO)**
On the industry's safety alignment: it's entirely plausible AI can be handled through shared standards and practices for the foreseeable future. Greater oversight, testing, liability and regulation are inevitable at some point in capability progress, but the industry can still align collectively — "awesome to see this come together so quickly... without reducing competition."
🔗 https://x.com/levie/status/2105111520913039530

谈行业安全共识：在可预见的未来，AI 完全可能靠一套共享标准和实践来管好安全问题。随着能力提升，更多监督、测试和责任与监管层迟早会来，但目前行业仍能自发对齐——"看到共识这么快形成、整个行业都支持进步且没有牺牲竞争，这预示着 AI 的未来要光明得多。"

**Nikunj Kothari (FPV Ventures partner)**
Two honest market observations: (1) On people — it's harder than ever to find missionaries. Cofounders leaving for "obvious" winners, public CEOs leaving for labs, companies getting "Windsurf-ed" where only the chosen join the future company. "It seems like a great time to be a mercenary, and everyone feels like one." Early-stage VCs bet on people, and when they leave on a dime, the company usually becomes a shell. (2) On defensibility — the moat is no longer "one" thing; in the app layer the harness is a thousand small things done well, much of it invisible, and you have to dig into the details to see why a company is different.
🔗 https://x.com/nikunj/status/2105148927599423752

两条对市场的诚实观察：其一论人——找"传教士"前所未有地难，联合创始人撂下自己创办的公司去投奔"明牌赢家"，上市公司 CEO 跑去 lab，公司被"Windsurf 式收购"后只有被挑中的人能进"未来的公司"。做早期 VC 本质是投人，人随时走掉，剩下的公司往往就只剩空壳。其二论护城河——在这个速度下，护城河不再是"某一个东西"，应用层的 harness 是一千件做好的小事，且大多不可见，必须深挖细节才能看出一家公司的真正差异。
