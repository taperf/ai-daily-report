**Training Data: Google's AI Infrastructure Chief, Amin Vahdat, on the Physics & Economics of Frontier AI**

The Takeaway: the biggest CapEx build-out in human history is being designed around a surprisingly durable bet — that the fundamental architecture of AI compute (TPU-style matrix engines, sparse cores, remote memory access) will stay stable for a decade while everything around it gets rebuilt.

Amin Vahdat, who took over Google's AI infrastructure at the end of last year, is spearheading a build-out Google is expected to spend over $200B on this year. Three insights stand out. First, on hardware strategy: specialization only pays off if the workload is durable — "if it's gonna go away after a month or two, even if it's big for those three months, you got a really narrow window to intercept it." The stunning fact is that TPU architecture at a medium level of detail hasn't really changed since TPU v1; like a CPU's instruction set, the primitives (large matrix multiply units, sparse cores, remote load/store over the ICI network) have survived many generations of models. Second, on the agent shift: long-horizon agents remove the human rate-limiter — interaction time goes from seconds to potentially days, which completely reshapes what data centers must sustain. Third, on 2036: he expects racks to be manufactured centrally at multiple megawatts each — you wheel one in, plug in water, power, and a small fiber bundle, "and you're off to the races" — with space-based data centers a real possibility (though cooling and repairs are actually harder in space, and free-space optics would replace fiber).

本期核心结论：人类历史上最大的资本开支建设，其实是围绕一个惊人稳定的判断在设计——AI 算力的基础架构（TPU 式矩阵引擎、稀疏核心、远程内存访问）将保持十年不变，而它周围的一切都会被重造。Amin Vahdat 去年底执掌 Google AI 基础设施，正在主导 Google 今年预计超 2000 亿美元的 CapEx 建设。三个洞察尤其值得记下。其一，硬件策略：专用化只有在工作负载足够持久时才划算——"如果它一两个月后就消失，哪怕那两个月再大，你能拦截到的窗口也太窄了"。惊人的事实是：TPU 架构在中层粒度上自 v1 以来几乎没变——像 CPU 指令集一样，大矩阵乘单元、稀疏核心、经 ICI 网络的远程读写这些原语，扛过了一代又一代模型。其二，agent 带来的转变：长时程 agent 去掉了"人在回路里"这个天然限流器——交互时长从几秒变成可能持续数天，数据中心需要承载的负载形态完全不同了。其三，关于 2036 年：他预计机架将集中制造、单架功率达数兆瓦——推进机房，接上水、电和一小束光纤，"就能开跑"——太空数据中心也真的在考虑范围内（虽然太空散热和维修其实更难，光纤会被自由空间激光光学取代）。
