### Aaron Levie — Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie posted a long-form thread arguing that some of the "hardest" work in the world is actually the most prone to automation — precisely because it's verifiable. Math, cybersecurity, and code, while insanely difficult and high-value, have the benefit of objective correctness tests. This gives AI training clearer reward signals and allows scalable verification of model outputs. Conversely, domains like legal negotiations, marketing campaigns, sales messaging, and financial planning lack instant verifiability — they depend on changing context, operator judgment, and risk tolerance. The implication: even as model capabilities increase exponentially, the applied AI layer will require significant innovation, and many workflows themselves will need to evolve to capture the full gains from automation.

🔗 https://x.com/levie/status/2083965372747882741

Box CEO Aaron Levie 发布长帖指出，世界上一些"最难"的工作实际上最容易被自动化——恰恰因为它们是可验证的。数学、网络安全和代码虽然极其困难且高价值，但拥有客观正确性测试的优势。这为 AI 训练提供了更清晰的奖励信号，并允许对模型输出进行可扩展的验证。相比之下，法律谈判、营销活动、销售话术和财务规划等领域缺乏即时可验证性——它们依赖于变化的背景、操作者的判断和风险偏好。这意味着：即使模型能力指数级增长，应用 AI 层仍需要大量创新，许多工作流程本身也需要进化才能充分获取自动化的收益。

### Jerry Tworek & Rohan Anil — Core Automation Co-founders

On the Training Data podcast, Core Automation co-founders Jerry Tworek (former VP at OpenAI, led Strawberry and Reasoning teams) and Rohan Anil (former Gemini pre-training lead, Google Brain) laid out their contrarian bet: the transformer architecture itself is the bottleneck to better AI. Tworek argued that we've mastered pre-training and RL at scale, but transformers can't learn continuously from deployment — they suffer from catastrophic forgetting and low data efficiency when fine-tuned, and in-context learning is limited to roughly 20 minutes of usage before compression is needed. Anil added that transformers have poor "computational depth" — they're shallow (at most ~100 layers) and autoregressive decoding is inherently inefficient, one token at a time. The duo believes big labs are trapped in a competitive cycle where scaling transformers for the next quarter takes priority over exploring alternatives. Core Automation's mission: find a new architecture that enables continuous learning, build the most automated lab to iterate fast, and write custom GPU kernels to make it efficient. Their six-month roadmap includes automating kernel generation — their recent QR kernel competition showed human+coding agents could achieve 60x speedups over cuSolver, but frontier LLMs couldn't solve the problem at all.

🔗 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2RJiaf0SY8s

在 Training Data 播客中，Core Automation 联合创始人 Jerry Tworek（前 OpenAI VP，领导过 Strawberry 和 Reasoning 团队）和 Rohan Anil（前 Gemini 预训练负责人，Google Brain）阐述了他们的逆向赌注：transformer 架构本身是更好 AI 的瓶颈。Tworek 认为我们已经掌握了大规模预训练和 RL，但 transformer 无法从部署中持续学习——微调时存在灾难性遗忘和低数据效率，而上下文学习在需要压缩前仅限约 20 分钟的使用量。Anil 补充说 transformer 的"计算深度"很差——它们很浅（最多约 100 层），自回归解码本质上是低效的，一次一个 token。两人认为大实验室被困在竞争循环中，为下一季度扩展 transformer 的优先级高于探索替代方案。Core Automation 的使命：找到一种支持持续学习的新架构，构建最自动化的实验室以快速迭代，并编写自定义 GPU kernel 以提高效率。他们的六个月路线图包括自动化 kernel 生成——最近的 QR kernel 竞赛显示人类+编码 agent 可实现比 cuSolver 60 倍的加速，但前沿 LLM 根本无法解决这个问题。

🔗 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2RJiaf0SY8s
