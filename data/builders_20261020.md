### Beam: The Great American Open Model — No Priors（嘉宾 Misha Laskin，Reflection AI 联合创始人 & CEO）

**The Takeaway:** Building a competitive Western open-model lab costs billions now and tens of billions next year — but "AI improving AI" is running at 30x annual efficiency gains, which is what keeps the whole arms race from collapsing under its own CapEx.

Misha Laskin — a former Google DeepMind reinforcement-learning researcher (PhD in Physics) who co-founded Reflection AI with Janus, a DeepMind founding engineer and key AlphaGo contributor — just shipped Beam, the company's first open model. His opening argument is the most candid case for open models in security: "When you remove cyber offensive capabilities, you also remove cyber defensive capabilities... A very powerful closed model went and hacked into another company, and the only way that company could remediate itself was by using open models to protect itself." His version of Linus' law: with enough eyeballs, most security and safety vulnerabilities become shallow too.

On why Reflection builds end-to-end: a year in, "all the good open models are coming from China. There were not really good Western open models," and they needed one as a base — plus the research reality that you must pre-train your own model to make RL work at scale. On capital requirements, his numbers are refreshingly concrete: catching up to the frontier cost "order of $100M" eighteen months ago, "single-digit billions" six months ago, and "tens of billions" going into next year — roughly a 4x compute multiplier per model generation (100k H100s → 100k Blackwells for Astra → 100k Vera Rubins next). Beam itself: 500B total parameters, 23B active, trained on 6,000 GB300s in about 12 days with infrastructure efficiencies, with RL taking slightly more — over 10,000 GB300s for four weeks. "There were more FLOPs spent on reinforcement learning."

The most striking claim of the episode: while human researchers deliver roughly 7x annual improvement in pre-training efficiency, "we're probably at a point where it's 30x efficiency, depending how good your model is for actually improving itself." Model-built-models as the compounding engine — and his answer to whether CapEx must asymptote: yes eventually, but intelligence-per-FLOP and revenue-per-FLOP keep rising, so the asymptote keeps moving out.

**核心观点：** 建一家有竞争力的西方开源模型实验室，现在的门槛是数十亿美元，明年是数百亿——但"AI 改进 AI"正以每年 30 倍的效率增益运转，这是整场军备竞赛没有在自己 CapEx 重压下崩塌的原因。

Misha Laskin——前 Google DeepMind 强化学习研究员（物理学博士），与 DeepMind 创始工程师、AlphaGo 核心贡献者 Janus 联合创立 Reflection AI——刚刚发布了公司首个开源模型 Beam。他开场的论证是对开源模型在安全层面最坦率的辩护："当你移除网络攻击能力时，你也移除了网络防御能力……一个强大的闭源模型去黑了另一家公司，而那家受害公司唯一能自救的方式，是用开源模型来保护自己。"他版的林纳斯定律： eyeballs 够多，大多数安全与对齐漏洞也会变浅。

关于为什么要端到端自建：创业一年后他发现"所有好的开源模型都来自中国，西方当时没有真正好的开源模型"，而他们需要一个基座——加上研究层面的现实：想让 RL 在规模上起作用，就必须自己预训练。关于资金门槛，他的数字异常具体：追赶到前沿的成本，十八个月前是"1 亿美元量级"，六个月前是"个位数十亿美元"，进入明年是"数百亿"——每代模型算力约 4 倍增长（10 万张 H100 → Astra 用的 10 万张 Blackwell → 下一代 10 万张 Vera Rubin）。Beam 本身：总参数 500B、激活 23B，用 6000 张 GB300 训练，靠基础设施优化约 12 天跑完；RL 部分反而更多——超过 10000 张 GB300 跑了四周。"花在强化学习上的 FLOPs 更 多。"

全期最惊人的论断：人类研究员每年在预训练效率上带来约 7 倍改进，而"我们现在大概到了 30 倍效率的节点，取决于你的模型自我改进做得有多好"。模型造模型作为复利引擎——至于 CapEx 是否必然到达渐近线：最终会，但每 FLOP 的智能量和每 FLOP 的收入都在涨，渐近线一直在往后挪。

### Anthropic Engineering：How we contain Claude across products

The engineering question is no longer whether to give Claude dangerous access — it's how to cap the blast radius. "Twelve months ago, we'd have rejected out of hand the idea of granting Claude access sufficient to take down an internal Anthropic service. Today that level of access is routine, and Anthropic developers are more productive for it." Two levers exist: human-in-the-loop supervision and containment. The human-in-the-loop data point is brutal: "Our telemetry showed users approved roughly 93% of permission prompts" — approval fatigue makes probabilistic supervision degrade over time, which motivated Claude Code auto mode. The post's focus is the second lever: supervising what the agent is able to do via sandboxes, VMs, and egress controls. Also on the record: Claude Mythos Preview was held back in April 2026 because its blast radius was deemed too high, with broader release expected as safeguards mature.

工程问题已经不再是"要不要给 Claude 危险权限"，而是"如何封顶爆炸半径"。"十二个月前，如果有人提议给 Claude 足以搞垮 Anthropic 内部服务的权限，我们会一口回绝。今天这个级别的权限已是日常，Anthropic 的开发者因此更高效。"文章指出两个杠杆：人机协同监督与遏制（containment）。人机协同的数据很残酷："我们的遥测显示，用户批准了大约 93% 的权限请求"——批准疲劳让概率性监督随时间退化，这催生了 Claude Code auto mode。文章重心在第二个杠杆：不监督 agent 做什么，而是通过沙箱、虚拟机和出口管控监督它"能做什么"。另外留档：Claude Mythos Preview 因爆炸半径过高在 2026 年 4 月被暂缓发布，随着防御方加固关键系统，更广泛的发布会变得合适。
