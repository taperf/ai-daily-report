## PODCASTS

**No Priors** — Rethinking Legacy Data Infrastructure with Eon Co-Founders Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein

🔗 https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast

**The Takeaway:** In the AI era, data — not models or compute — is becoming the true moat. But enterprise data is scattered, locked, and increasingly under threat from both human and non-human actors.

Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein, co-founders of EON (a cloud backup and data recovery service rebuilt for the AI era), argue that models and compute are increasingly commoditized with near-zero switching costs. What truly differentiates companies now is their proprietary data. They point to Google's $10 million purchase of Spirit Airlines' data out of bankruptcy — not its planes — as a signal of how valuable real-world enterprise datasets have become for training AI models.

A major theme throughout the conversation is the security implications of AI agents. Unlike traditional threats from human attackers, agents operate with legitimate permissions and can cause damage at extreme velocity — dropping database tables, mishandling sensitive data, or creating "non-human identities" that are nearly impossible to track. The founders note that just months ago, few leaders discussed this; now, almost every enterprise executive they meet has either experienced an agent-related incident or fears one.

On the enterprise stack, they predict existing ETL and data engineering infrastructure will be upended because agents generate data differently than humans do — more dynamically, across more systems, with less predictable patterns. Tools like Fivetran and DBT, while excellent for their original purposes, were built for a world where data teams had single, well-defined purposes. In the agentic era, companies need to continuously discover, classify, and make accessible all their data without compromising security or production uptime.

The discussion also touches on a broader shift in how companies consume AI software: "forward deployed engineers" — once a niche practice — are now becoming standard as enterprises desperate to adopt AI bring in experienced engineers with Silicon Valley toolboxes to transform legacy organizations. And some companies, like Long Lake, are taking an even more radical approach: buying traditional companies outright and converting them into AI-native operations.

---

**核心要点：** 在 AI 时代，数据——而非模型或算力——正在成为真正的护城河。但企业数据分散、被锁定，且正面临来自人类和非人类行为者的双重威胁。

EON 联合创始人 Ofir Ehrlich 和 Gonen Stein（一家为 AI 时代重建的云备份与数据恢复服务）认为，模型和算力正日益商品化，切换成本几乎为零。现在真正区分公司价值的是其专有数据。他们以 Google 在 Spirit Airlines 破产时斥资 1000 万美元收购其数据（而非飞机）为例，说明真实企业数据集对训练 AI 模型的价值已达到何种程度。

整场对话的一个核心主题是 AI agent 的安全影响。与传统的人类攻击者威胁不同，agent 以合法权限运行，能以极快的速度造成损害——删除数据表、 mishandle 敏感数据、或创建几乎无法追踪的"非人类身份"。创始人指出，就在几个月前，还很少有高管讨论这个问题；而现在，他们遇到的几乎每位企业高管要么经历过 agent 相关事件，要么对此深感担忧。

在企业数据栈方面，他们预测现有的 ETL 和数据工程基础设施将被颠覆，因为 agent 生成数据的方式与人类不同——更动态、跨更多系统、模式更不可预测。Fivetran 和 DBT 等工具虽然在其原始场景下非常出色，但它们是为数据团队有明确单一目标的世界而建的。在 agent 时代，公司需要持续发现、分类并使其所有数据可访问，同时不牺牲安全性或生产稳定性。

对话还涉及公司采用 AI 软件的更广泛转变："前置部署工程师"——曾经是一种小众做法——现在正成为标准，因为急于采用 AI 的企业引入拥有硅谷工具箱的经验丰富的工程师来改造传统组织。而一些公司，如 Long Lake，则采取了更激进的方式：直接收购传统公司并将其转化为 AI 原生运营。
