### 核心观点速览（双语）

**The Takeaway / 一句话总结**

Transformers have hit a ceiling — not because of scale, but because they cannot learn continuously. The next leap in AI requires a fundamentally new architecture that learns at test time, on real-world data, from users.

Transformer 已触及天花板——不是因为规模不足，而是因为它无法持续学习。AI 的下一个跃升需要一个全新的架构，能够在测试时、在真实世界数据上、从用户那里持续学习。

---

**Jerry Tworek 对 Transformer 的\u201c悼词\u201d**

Tworek 在播客中解释了他那条引发热议的推文：\u201c替换 Transformer 的第一步，是深刻理解它曾带我们走了多远。\u201d 他认为，当前行业最大的瓶颈不是算力或数据，而是**架构本身**。过去六年，所有人都在同一列火车上——不断增加参数，本质上只做两件事：MOE（混合专家）和注意力机制。现在，是时候重新思考了。

Tworek explains his viral tweet about replacing transformers: \u201cThe first step is appreciating deeply how far they were able to carry us.\u201d He argues the industry\u2019s bottleneck is the architecture itself — not compute or data. For six years, everyone rode the same train: adding more parameters, essentially doing two operations (MOE and attention). Now it\u2019s time to rethink.

---

**强化学习的局限性**

Tworek 坦承自己曾是\u201c强化学习最大化主义者\u201d，他曾认为只要大规模扩展 RL，2025年就能实现AGI。但他亲眼见证了模型在 benchmark 上不断刷新的同时，真实世界的任务依然无法解决。核心问题：**训练数据和评估基准是一枚硬币的两面**，但真实世界的分布远比训练数据更混乱、更不可预测。

Tworek admits he was an \u201cRL maximalist\u201d who believed scaling reinforcement learning would solve everything by 2025. But he watched models crush benchmarks while real-world tasks remained unsolved. The core problem: training data and eval benchmarks are two sides of the same coin, but the real world is far messier and more unpredictable.

---

**\u201c计算深度\u201d才是Transformer的真正软肋**

Rohan Anil 指出，大多数 Transformer 非常浅——最多100层。虽然\u201c思维链\u201d和推理时扩展增加了计算深度，但代价巨大：模型必须逐token生成，效率极低。他提出一个根本性问题：如果优化目标是更好的架构，必须同时考虑预训练和RL，找到比\u201c逐token生成\u201d更高效的计算方式。

Rohan Anil points out most transformers are quite shallow — at most ~100 layers. While chain-of-thought and inference-time scaling add computational depth, the cost is enormous: models must generate one token at a time, which is deeply inefficient. His core question: if we want a better architecture, we must optimize pre-training and RL together, finding ways to spend compute more efficiently than autoregressive token generation.

---

**为什么离开大厂，自己干？**

Tworek 认为，当前最大的实验室正处于史上最激烈的市场竞争中，这让他们无暇尝试替代路径。\u201c如果Transformer profitable，你能花资源在下个季度赢，就很难把精力投入到一两年后才能见效的新方向上。\u201d 这正是 Core Automation 想填补的生态位。

Tworek explains why he left Big Tech: the largest labs are in the most competitive market fight ever, leaving no bandwidth for alternative paths. \u201cIf transformers are profitable and you can spend resources scaling them to win next quarter, it\u2019s very hard to put energy into something that might redefine the field in a year or two.\u201d This gap is what Core Automation aims to fill.

---

**自动化实验室的愿景**

Core Automation 的目标是成为\u201c世界上最自动化的实验室\u201d——不是把人踢出循环，而是给每个人最大的能力杠杆。Tworek 形容：走路能到的地方有限，骑车能到更远，开车能到更远——他们想让每个研究员都能\u201c开车\u201d。目标是每天能执行至少一个实验，甚至10个、100个。

The goal is the \u201cmost automated lab in the world\u201d — not removing humans from the loop, but giving each researcher maximum leverage. Tworek\u2019s analogy: walking gets you somewhere, biking gets you farther, driving gets you much farther. They want every researcher to be able to \u201cdrive.\u201d The target: at least one experiment per day, eventually ten or a hundred.

---

**AGI 的定义**

Tworek 对AGI有清醒而独特的定义：不是\u201c在经济上有价值的工作中超越所有人类\u201d，而是\u201c能够在没有人类介入的情况下自我改进的模型\u201d。他坦言，目前还没有任何任务能让LLM完全脱离人类循环——\u201c人类-LLM混合体非常成功，但LLM单独干，不太行。\u201d

Tworek\u2019s definition of AGI: not \u201coutperforming all humans in economically valuable work,\u201d but \u201ca model that can improve itself without humans in the loop.\u201d He\u2019s blunt: no task exists where LLMs have removed humans entirely. \u201cThe human-LLM hybrid is really successful. LLMs without humans? Not so much. Not at all.\u201d

---

**\u201c预训练+RL必须端到端优化\u201d**

Anil 认为行业犯了一个根本错误：把预训练和强化学习当成两个独立问题。预训练优化的是perplexity（困惑度），但RL一进来就把这些指标全部推翻。真正的问题是：我们训练这些模型到底是为了什么？必须从端到端的结果出发，而不是优化中间指标。

Anil argues the industry makes a fundamental mistake by treating pre-training and RL as separate problems. Pre-training optimizes perplexity, but RL destroys those metrics the moment it starts. The real question: what are we training these models for? We must optimize end-to-end outcomes, not intermediate metrics.

---

**内核生成的自动化竞赛**

Rohan Anil 透露，Core Automation 举办了一场 QR 分解内核竞赛。用现有工具（如CuSolver）只能获得基准性能；人类+搜索循环能提升7倍；但顶尖人类专家花4周、10万美元预算，能让代码快60倍。而当前所有前沿模型（OpenAI、Anthropic、Gemini）都无法解决这个级别的内核优化问题。这正是他们想要自动化的核心瓶颈。

Anil reveals Core Automation hosted a QR decomposition kernel competition. Existing tools get baseline performance; human + search loop gets 7x; but top human experts spending $100K over 4 weeks achieve 60x speedup. And current frontier models cannot solve this level of kernel optimization. This is the core bottleneck they aim to automate.
