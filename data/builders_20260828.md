**Claude Blog: Claude gets its own browser in Cowork**

Claude now has a built-in browser in the desktop app's Cowork feature. When a task involves a website, a browser opens in the side panel and Claude handles navigation, reading, clicking, and typing. **AI & I by Every — A $10B Hedge Fund's AI Playbook (Best of the Pod)**

*The Takeaway:* A $10B AUM hedge fund CEO named Will is leading one of the most aggressive organizational AI transformations in finance — not by forcing adoption, but by building conviction from the top down and treating AI literacy as a core competency for every employee.

Will, CEO/CIO of Walleye Capital (close to $10B AUM), is not your typical podcast guest — he's deliberately stepping into the spotlight because he feels "it would be irresponsible of me not to go after it with the maximum amount of discipline and intensity I can muster." A self-described "bonafide nerd" (Princeton engineer, Oxford math PhD), he started his career writing code for algorithmic trading strategies.

The journey started two years ago when an analyst built an internal tool called "Current" using early GPT-3 to make himself more efficient — eventually aiming to replace his own job as an analyst. Will's reaction: "Wow, this is where we're going." Today, Current is used by all of Walleye's portfolio managers and over 50 external firms have asked to beta test it.

Will's AI-first memo is worth quoting directly: "Using ChatGPT is not cheating. That's a non-applicable idea from academia. I use ChatGPT to write this email. You should be using it too and be proud of it. As a hedge fund, we should be ashamed to leave money on the table by ignoring tools that make us faster, smarter, and more effective. Not using these tools is like refusing to use the Internet in 1995 because it wasn't perfect."

Key operational moves: mandatory AI training for all 400 employees regardless of technical background or department; encouraging LLM use across accounting, finance, compliance, and legal; recording essentially all information flowing through the firm (the "Borg"); and using AI for his own daily journaling — capturing concepts from his day in 30 seconds, which the machine then processes in his voice.

On the human-AI balance, he's clear-eyed: "These tools don't negate the necessity to think. If anything, they should just give you more time to think... In an ideal case, someone has clearly used these tools, but the concepts still come from them... There's that optimal point on the curve that we're trying to get to."
