### Aaron Levie (CEO, Box)

Box CEO Aaron Levie shared a deep thread on why **Field Development Engineers (FDEs) are real, critical, and not going away anytime soon** in the AI era. His core argument: AI agents are fundamentally different from traditional software because they introduce non-deterministic, rapidly evolving systems into workflows that have never been automated before. "If you're building an AI agent for accounting in 2026, there is no established workflow, because literally nobody has ever used one of these. Nobody knows what the user journey looks like — not you, and importantly, not your customer either." This means constant evals, heavy customization, model updates, and harness changes — creating sustained demand for the human engineers who bridge the gap between AI capabilities and enterprise reality.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 深入阐述了为何 **Field Development Engineer（FDE）在 AI 时代是真实存在、至关重要且短期内不会消失的**。他的核心论点是：AI agent 与传统软件的根本不同在于，它将非确定性的、快速演进的系统引入了从未被自动化过的工作流。"如果你在 2026 年做一个会计 AI agent，根本不存在既定工作流，因为 literally 没人用过这东西。用户旅程长什么样——你不知道，更重要的是，你的客户也不知道。"这意味着需要持续跑 eval、重度定制、跟进模型更新、调整系统 harness——由此创造了对 FDE 的持续需求，他们需要弥合 AI 能力与企业现实之间的鸿沟。

The company serves physical operations — construction, energy utilities, logistics — with a full-stack platform of hardware sensors (GPS trackers, dashcams, asset tags), cloud services, and now AI agents. Biswas's core insight is that physical AI is fundamentally different from digital AI because the physical world isn't already digitized. "These are not the tokens you're gonna find online. Biswas also makes a bold prediction about the future of work: the data center boom is driving massive infrastructure demand (one utility plans to triple grid capacity in 5 years versus 125 years of historical build), and trades like electricians are in incredible demand. "The trades are in incredible demand. Their jobs are getting more modernized as well."

On autonomous vehicles, he believes robotaxis will scale faster than autonomous trucking because commercial vehicles are far more diverse and specialized than sedans — cement mixers, garbage trucks, and field service vans each require custom autonomy solutions, making adoption a 10-20 year diffusion curve rather than an overnight revolution.
