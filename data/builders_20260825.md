### Box CEO Aaron Levie: Why zero data retention (ZDR) is silently driving AI adoption

In a separate thread, Levie made the contrarian case that **zero data retention (ZDR) is responsible for a substantial amount of AI growth** because it dramatically simplified the compliance process most companies face when handling data with subprocessors. For applied AI tools driving growth, ZDR agreements are the default — anything else triggers an exception process that "takes far longer." Inside enterprises, most companies have internal governance requiring ZDR models because they can't explicitly separate PII and sensitive data in context windows. Levie's conclusion: "Without ZDR, AI diffusion grinds to a halt."

在另一条推文中，Levie 提出了一个反直觉的观点：**零数据保留（ZDR）正在默默推动 AI 的大规模采用**，因为它极大简化了企业在使用子处理器处理数据时的合规流程。对于推动增长的 AI 应用工具，ZDR 协议是默认要求——任何例外都会触发"耗时更长的"审批流程。在企业内部，大多数公司的内部治理要求使用 ZDR 模型，因为它们无法在上下文窗口中明确区分 PII 和敏感数据。Levie 的结论："没有 ZDR，AI 扩散将陷入停滞。"

### Meta Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru: The discriminatory property of evals

Madhu Guru published Part 8 of his "How to build great evals" series, focusing on a subtle but critical concept: **the discriminatory power of evaluations**. Using the analogy of giving a fifth-grade math test to PhDs, he explained that an eval is only useful if it can meaningfully separate AI systems of different capabilities. The sweet spot is "realistic + difficult + sensitive to differences in capability" — not so easy that everyone aces it, not so hard that everyone fails. He also posed an open question to the community: what do you do when your good evals saturate as your harness and models improve?

Meta AI 高级总监 Madhu Guru 发布了"如何构建优质评估"系列的第八部分，聚焦于一个微妙但关键的概念：**评估的区分度（discriminatory power）**。他用"给博士生做五年级数学题"的类比解释，只有当评估能够有意义地区分不同能力水平的 AI 系统时才有价值。最佳点是"真实场景 + 有难度 + 对能力差异敏感"——不能太简单以至于人人满分，也不能太难以至于人人不及格。他还向社区抛出一个开放问题：当好的评估随着 harness 和模型的进步而饱和时，你该怎么办？
