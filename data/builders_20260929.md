### Training Data: Box's Aaron Levie — On Reinventing Yourself in the AI Age and Enterprise Diffusion

**The Takeaway:** The trillion-dollar question in AI isn't whether models get smarter, it's who bridges the vast gap between model capability and enterprise workflow — and Levie is betting that gap is so deep that the application layer, not the labs, captures most of the value.

**EN remix:** Box CEO Aaron Levie, whose company sits on hundreds of billions of enterprise files, laid out why he thinks the "model wrapper" stigma is dead. His core argument: a trillion dollars has effectively been bet on one of two outcomes — either the bridge between model and workflow is limited, or it's vast and deep. He's certain it's the latter. Like AWS creating trillions in infrastructure value while Snowflake and Databricks built trillions on top, models will be insanely valuable, but "the application of bringing those models into real workflows in banking and life sciences and healthcare and government, that's just going to be a lot of software."

He's equally blunt on why enterprises won't trust the labs to run their agents: "You would want the task to be cost optimized... It's the company that doesn't care among 10 different models which model is performing that task." Model subsidization, he predicts, is temporary — once labs go public, "they will be held to basically the same laws of capitalism that everybody else is." And with Meta, SpaceX, China, and even NVIDIA willing to run inference at 10% margins, token costs keep falling, which means more value accrues to the application layer.

On AI diffusion, his explanation for why coding went vertical while everything else crawls is the best I've heard: coding is pure text, models are hyper-trained on it, and its audience is the most technical on the planet — while a sales rep's value creation is rate-limited by whether a customer answers the phone. "There's no 'give us your GitHub' for knowledge work." His prediction: in five years, 90% of enterprise tokens will be spent on tasks a user never kicked off. The winner will be whoever masters enterprise diffusion, not model capability.

> Most memorable quote: "You're either a year ahead or a year behind simply based on your feed." — on why every college student should be on AI Twitter

Box CEO Aaron Levie 掌握着数千亿企业文件，他解释了为什么"套壳"的污名已经死了。核心论点：市场上实际上有万亿美元押注在两种结局上——要么模型与工作流之间的桥很短，要么很长很深。他笃定是后者。正如 AWS 创造了数万亿基础设施价值，Snowflake 和 Databricks 又在其上建起数万亿市值；模型会极其有价值，但"把模型带进银行、生命科学、医疗、政府真实工作流的应用，会是海量的软件"。

他对"企业为什么不信任实验室来跑自己的 Agent"同样直白："你希望任务是成本最优化的……你应该选的是那家在 10 个模型里根本不在乎用哪个来执行任务的公司。"他预测模型补贴只是暂时现象——实验室一旦上市，"就得遵守和其他所有人一样的资本主义法则"。而 Meta、SpaceX、中国甚至 NVIDIA 都愿意以 10% 的毛利跑推理，token 成本会持续下降，价值将进一步涌向应用层。
