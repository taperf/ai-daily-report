## PODCASTS

**AI & I by Every** — "Why the Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social" (Best of the Pod)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlI-5W7d7uU

**The Takeaway:** The next breakthrough AI consumer product will look less like ChatGPT (a solo utility) and more like Pinterest or Instagram—a social, multiplayer experience with network effects and UGC.

Benchmark partner and former Pinterest early employee Sarah Tavel argues that AI's consumer wins so far follow an old pattern: ChatGPT resembles Google, built by deeply technical founders with a simple text-box interface. But as technology matures, the slider moves toward product thinkers and social experiences. Custom GPTs, in her view, "feel criminal"—made by an unbelievably capable team that fundamentally doesn't understand social.

The real opportunity? A UGC community where skilled users create and share, making AI accessible to everyone else through network effects. Tavel envisions a future where you visit "Sarah's GPT" (or some network equivalent), input personal data like blood tests and family info, and get personalized outputs—potentially cannibalizing general-purpose tools like ChatGPT over time. The knowledge of how to build with AI, she argues, won't spread through blogs and podcasts alone; it needs a social substrate to propagate.

On investing through hype cycles, Tavel says Benchmark always starts with "is this a company we wanna work with?" Price matters, but when they believe in unlimited potential, they make it work. Her taste in founders: those for whom entrepreneurship is an "affliction"—a rash they have to scratch, not a cool new job. The relentlessness of the role demands learning machines who put the company's best interest above their own ego.

真正的机会在哪里？一个 UGC 社区，熟练的用户创造和分享内容，通过网络效应让 AI 对所有人变得可用。Tavel 设想了一个未来：你访问"Sarah 的 GPT"（或某种网络等效物），输入个人数据如血检结果和家庭信息，获得个性化输出——久而久之可能蚕食 ChatGPT 这样的通用工具。她认为，AI 构建知识不会仅靠博客和播客传播；它需要一个社交基底来扩散。

关于在炒作周期中投资，Tavel 说 Benchmark 总是从"这是我们想合作的公司吗？"开始。价格重要，但当他们相信潜力无限时，他们会想办法促成。她对创始人的品味：那些把创业当作" affliction（执念）"的人——一种必须去挠的痒，而不是一份很酷的新工作。这份工作的无情要求创始人是"学习机器"，把公司的最佳利益置于自我之上。
