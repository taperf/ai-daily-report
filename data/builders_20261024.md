**Training Data：David Silver 离开 DeepMind 创办 Ineffable——一个不训练在人类数据上的“超级学习者”**

*The Takeaway:* The team that cracked superhuman Go believes the fastest path to superintelligence is giving up human data entirely — and David Silver now predicts strong evidence within a year and true superintelligence in about three.

David Silver — the researcher behind AlphaGo and AlphaZero — and Lasse Espeholt, whose large-scale RL systems (IMPALA, SeedRL) much of today's field traces back to, have left DeepMind to found Ineffable Intelligence, a lab built on a single bet: superintelligence will come not from scaling LLMs on human data, but from a "super learner" — a system that discovers knowledge entirely on its own, starting from nothing, the way humanity collectively discovered language, science, and mathematics without any blueprint.

The core argument is the AlphaGo-to-AlphaZero arc. AlphaGo took two years of human data and human-in-the-loop engineering to beat Lee Sedol. AlphaZero gave up human data entirely — and took four hours to surpass the best chess engine ever built. Silver's conclusion: lean on human data and you inherit human priors, capping what your system can ever discover. "Thinking about these things in terms of language might not even be the right paradigm — all of this human data may be weighing things down." The lab's answer is "crucibles": simulated environments packed with emergent complexity (physics engines, millions of interacting agents) where agents with simple objectives can rediscover — or go beyond — the entire human journey of discovery. The case for simulation: a Turing-complete simulator can express everything we mean by computation, economics, language, and mathematics, and it is infinitely parallelizable, reproducible, and the safest place to develop what he calls the "science of superintelligence" — including alignment research he believes can no longer rely on monitoring English chains of thought.

The most striking forecasts: extremely strong evidence the approach works within a year, and true superintelligence — discovering things far beyond human knowledge — in about three years, pulled in from his earlier 3-5 year estimate thanks to coding agents accelerating research. Compute is the enabler: by year-end Ineffable becomes the first customer of the largest GPU cluster on GCP, while the team stays deliberately small at 20 people — unusually high compute per person.

*最值得记住的一句话：* "Everything we learned, we discovered for ourselves — and that's an incredible thing that no one has cracked yet in AI. To me, that is the essence of what it will take to get to superintelligence."（人类的所有知识都是自己发现出来的——这是 AI 至今无人破解的奇迹，也是通往超级智能的本质。）

**中文摘要：**

AlphaGo 与 AlphaZero 幕后的 David Silver，和写出 IMPALA、SeedRL 等大规模强化学习系统的 Lasse Espeholt，双双离开 DeepMind 创办了 Ineffable Intelligence。这家公司的核心赌注只有一条：超级智能不会来自在人类数据上规模化的 LLM，而会来自一个“超级学习者”——一个从零开始、完全自主发现知识的系统，就像人类祖先没有任何蓝图，却集体发现了语言、科学与数学。最有说服力的论据是 AlphaGo 到 AlphaZero 的跨越：前者靠人类数据和人在回路，花了两年才战胜李世石；后者彻底放弃人类数据后，4 小时就超越史上最强的国际象棋引擎。Silver 的判断是：只要依赖人类数据，就会继承人类先验、封死发现的上限，“用语言来思考可能根本不是正确的范式，这些人类数据甚至可能是一种拖累”。

为此他们正在搭建“crucible（熔炉）”——具备大量涌现复杂度的模拟环境（物理引擎、百万级交互 agent），让只有简单目标的 agent 自主重走甚至超越人类的发现之旅。他论证了模拟的充分性：图灵完备的模拟器足以表达计算、经济、语言与数学的一切，且模拟可无限并行、可复现，也是最安全的场所——他相信对齐研究不能再依赖监控英文思维链，需要真正的“超级智能科学”。最惊人的是预测：一年内拿到方法可行性的强证据，三年内实现真正的超级智能——比他此前 3-5 年的估计提前，原因是 coding agent 加速了研究。算力是底牌：年底前他们将成为 GCP 上最大 GPU 集群的首个客户，团队刻意保持在 20 人，人均算力极高。
