### Latent Space × Gabe Greenberg（G2i CEO）：拆解 AI 训练数据市场

**EN**
The Takeaway: the human-data industry behind frontier labs has quietly scaled from gig work to expert-driven data engineering — and it now runs on about five customers.

Gabe Greenberg, CEO of G2i, is remixing the training-data supply chain for frontier labs: human data and computer-use infrastructure to help labs train models, now branching into 3D, STEM, and mechanical engineering. The numbers are startling: $15M revenue last year to roughly $100M this year, no VC backing, profitable — with "five or six customers," essentially the frontier labs. He revealed that only one lab historically had a truly mature in-house human data operation; the rest leaned on off-the-shelf data, but partnerships are deepening as benchmarks get harder. The work itself has transformed: the RLHF era of low-paid gig labelers has given way to staff-level engineers doing complex dataset building with "taste work," and demand follows a Jevons paradox — the more models get used, the more training data they need. Most counterintuitive claim: SWE-bench, the industry's top coding benchmark for over a year, "was like an undergrad project" with flawed instructions from the get-go — proof that "anything that gets a lot of attention doesn't need to be good." On eval data design: "If you make tasks too hard, they become evals; too easy, they're worthless. Make them just hard enough that they're a few steps from what the model can do today — trainable, and efficiently trainable."

**中文**
一句话核心：支撑前沿实验室的人类数据产业，已悄然从零工经济进化为专家驱动的数据工程——而它只服务于大约五个客户。

G2i CEO Gabe Greenberg 正在为前沿实验室重构训练数据供应链：人类数据 + computer use 基础设施，并向 3D、STEM、机械工程延伸。数字惊人：去年营收 1500 万美元，今年约 1 亿美元，无 VC 融资、已盈利——客户只有"五六个"，即那几家前沿实验室。他透露：真正拥有成熟 in-house 人类数据团队的实验室历史上只有一家，其余都依赖现成数据；随着 benchmark 越来越难，实验室与供应商正转向深度共创。数据工作本身也变了：RLHF 时代低价外包标注的零工模式，已被 staff 级工程师主导的复杂数据集构建取代，还讲究"品味"；需求呈杰文斯悖论——模型用得越多，要的训练数据就越多。最具反差的观点：称霸行业一年多的 SWE-bench "就是个本科生项目"，指令设计从根上有洞——"任何东西只要关注度够高，并不需要真的好"。关于评测数据的设计心法："任务出太难就变成了 eval，太容易就毫无价值；要刚好比模型今天能做到的难几步——可训练，且训练效率高。"
