### Every CEO Dan Shipper

Every CEO Dan Shipper published a detailed critique of a recent AI-and-jobs analysis, challenging two key assumptions: that jobs can be decomposed into discrete tasks, and that automation always reduces human labor. Shipper argues automation often creates multiples more work for humans, and draws on Wittgenstein and Heidegger to make the case that a job is "a particular way of seeing and caring about the world" — something that evolves and generates new tasks as tools and circumstances change, fundamentally missing from current economic models.
🔗 https://x.com/danshipper/status/2097758891270697101

Every CEO Dan Shipper 对近期一份 AI 与就业分析报告发表了详细批评，挑战了两个核心假设：一是工作可以被拆解为离散任务，二是自动化总会减少人类劳动。Shipper 认为自动化往往会成倍增加人类的工作量，并借用维特根斯坦和海德格尔的哲学来论证：一份工作"是一种看待世界和关怀世界的特定方式"——它会随着工具和情境的变化而演化，产生新的任务，而这在当前的经济模型中完全缺失。
