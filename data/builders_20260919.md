### 1. Claude Projects 正式发布：Anthropic 多位核心成员集中官宣（今日头条事件）

Anthropic rolled out the new Claude Projects experience, and this is the biggest builder story of the day. The official @claudeai account laid out the mechanics: projects build over time, every thread adds to and draws from shared memory (so Claude remembers "the release moved to Friday" or "who to ask before touching the billing service"), and a project library keeps both your files and the ones Claude creates. Threads run in the cloud so they keep working when your computer is offline, though local file and network access is "coming very soon." Existing Pro and Max projects keep working and will be upgraded as the rollout expands; there's a waitlist for earlier access.

Boris Cherny (Claude Code, Anthropic) gave the most personal endorsement: "I stopped managing sessions. I just send thoughts as they come, Claude splits them into threads, and the project remembers how I work. It's where I do a ton of my coding now." His tweet pulled 2,300+ likes and 200+ replies.

Cat Wu (claude code + cowork, Anthropic) explained the deeper design: Claude coordinates all of your sessions, so you operate at a higher level of abstraction. "I fire off a batch of tasks and move on. Claude has context on everything I'm working on and can give me an aggregated status update whenever I ask. It also has long-lived memory that evolves as I use it."

Thariq (Claude Code, Anthropic) added the architectural detail: Projects brings the architecture of Claude Tag to Claude Code, with one agent per project that manages memory and spins off subagents for tasks. You can ask it to be proactive or do things on a schedule.

Anthropic 正式推出全新 Claude Projects 体验，是今天 Builder 圈最大的事件。官方账号讲清了机制：项目随时间积累，每条对话都读写共享记忆（所以 Claude 会记得"发布改到周五了"或"动计费服务前要先问谁"），项目库同时保存你上传的文件和 Claude 创建的文件。对话线程跑在云端，电脑离线也能继续工作，本地文件和内网访问"很快支持"。现有 Pro 和 Max 项目不受影响，会随灰度扩大逐步升级，也可加入 waitlist 提前体验。

Boris Cherny（Anthropic Claude Code 负责人）给出了最个人化的背书："我不再管理会话了。想到什么就发什么，Claude 自动拆分成线程，项目会记住我的工作方式。现在我大量代码都在这里写。"这条推文获得 2300+ 赞和 200+ 回复。

Cat Wu（Anthropic claude code + cowork）解释了更深层的设计：Claude 统一协调你的所有会话，让你在更高的抽象层级工作。"我批量丢一组任务就去干别的，Claude 掌握我手头所有工作的上下文，随时能给我汇总状态。它还有会随使用进化的长期记忆。"

Thariq（Anthropic Claude Code）补充了架构细节：Projects 把 Claude Tag 的架构带到了 Claude Code，每个项目一个 agent，负责管理记忆并为任务派生子 agent，还可以让它主动做事、按计划执行。

### 2. Google Labs 发布 CC 家庭版：AI agent 开始接管家庭后勤

Google Labs announced the new CC, an AI agent built for families to "spend less time on logistics and more time together." You can add up to 5 family members to one agent, get a shared "Your Day Ahead" morning brief email, autosync schedules and to-dos with Google Calendar and Tasks, coordinate in Google Chat to offload tasks like weekly meal plans and school supply lists, and even delegate paperwork (permission slips, forms) for CC to complete under your direction. Notably, CC distinguishes what applies to everyone (family grocery lists) from what applies to one person (dietary restrictions, timezones). US only, 18+, waitlist open.

Josh Woodward (VP, Google Labs / Gemini App / Google AI Studio) amplified the launch twice, saying "Have a family? We ❤️ this in ours" and pointing to a user story about Gemini Notebook on mobile.

Google Labs 发布家庭版 AI agent「CC」，目标是"让家庭少花时间处理琐事、多花时间相处"。一个 agent 最多可添加 5 位家庭成员，每天早上发共享的「Your Day Ahead」简报邮件，自动同步 Google 日历和任务清单，可在 Google Chat 里让 CC 承接做饭计划、采购清单等事项，甚至可以把签许可条、填表格这类文书工作委托给 CC 在你的指示下完成。值得关注的一个细节：CC 能区分对全家生效的信息（家庭采购清单）和对个人生效的信息（饮食禁忌、时区）。目前仅限美国、18+，waitlist 已开放。

Josh Woodward（Google Labs / Gemini App / Google AI Studio 副总裁）连发两条助推，说"家里有娃的都会喜欢这个"，还转推了一个用户在手机上用 Gemini Notebook 的真实故事。

### 8. Nikunj Kothari, Partner at FPV Ventures

Nikunj Kothari (FPV Ventures partner) published a hands-on experiment in running a "self-driving company": nosugarforkids.com, a catalog of healthy snacks for kids that he built with "Claude in the loop" after trying many similar products and finding none met his bar. The autonomous side is the interesting part: a Claude agent wakes up once a day to check the catalog for new additions, prune dead products, find content ideas, check SEO performance data, write and edit new pieces, keep quality high ("tries to not introduce slop"), and now even reach out to publications for backlinks. With zero backlinks and no social presence, the site has organically grown to ~6,000 impressions a day and 60 clicks. He also shipped a full MCP and WebMCP for the site "if you want to direct your agent towards it." One of the clearest public walk-throughs yet of a one-person, agent-operated business.

Nikunj Kothari（FPV Ventures 合伙人）公开了一个「自动驾驶公司」的实操实验：nosugarforkids.com，一个儿童健康零食目录站。他试过市面上很多同类产品都不满意，于是自己带着 Claude 动手做了一个。更有意思的是自动化部分：一个 Claude agent 每天醒来执行一整套运营，检查目录新增、清理失效商品、找内容选题、查 SEO 表现数据、撰写和编辑新文章、控制质量（"尽量不制造 slop"），现在甚至开始主动联系媒体要外链。在零外链、零社媒存在的条件下，这个站自然增长到每天约 6000 次展示、60 次点击。他还给网站做了一个完整的 MCP 和 WebMCP，"方便你把 agent 指向它"。这是目前把单人加 agent 运营公司讲得最清楚的公开案例之一。
