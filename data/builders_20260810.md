## PODCASTS

**No Priors — "Chasing Trillion-Dollar Companies, Founder Ambition, Token Budgets, and Regulatory Capture"**
Sarah Guo and Elad Gil dive into the manic energy gripping AI right now. Elad's core thesis: we've seen an unprecedented three companies go from near-zero to trillion-dollar valuations in roughly five years (Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceX), but don't expect that velocity to repeat for multiple new entrants in the next 3–5 years. He argues that while plenty of $100B companies can still be built, multi-trillion-dollar outcomes require $50–100B revenue streams, and those markets are rare.

Elad also flags a concerning trend: some of the best founders are retreating to niche markets out of fear of the Neo Labs, when they should be competing head-on. His advice on exits: every company should have a pre-scheduled board conversation every six months about whether it's the right time to sell — because in AI, "every year is like three to four years of normal cycle time." The most underrated cost isn't financial dilution, it's the opportunity cost of a founder's most productive years.

On regulation, Elad draws a parallel to the safety lobby that killed nuclear energy in the 1970s: "70% of France is still nuclear… where are all the accidents?" He warns that regulatory capture and one-sided risk focus could slow AI progress dramatically, even as the upside potential for healthcare, education, and productivity is enormous.

**No Priors — "追逐万亿公司、创始人野心、Token 预算与监管捕获"**
Sarah Guo 和 Elad Gil 深入探讨了当前笼罩 AI 领域的狂热氛围。Elad 的核心论点：过去五年我们见证了史无前例的三家公司从接近零到万亿市值（Anthropic、OpenAI、SpaceX），但不要期望这种速度在未来 3–5 年内对多个新进入者重复。他认为虽然仍有大量 1000 亿美元级别的公司可以诞生，但万亿级别的结果需要 500–1000 亿美元的收入流，而这样的市场非常稀少。

Elad 还指出了一个令人担忧的趋势：一些最优秀的创始人正因为害怕 Neo Labs 而退缩到利基市场，而他认为他们应该正面竞争。关于退出策略，他的建议是：每家公司都应该每六个月进行一次预先安排的董事会讨论——因为在 AI 领域，"每一年都相当于正常周期的三到四年"。最被低估的成本不是财务稀释，而是创始人最富生产力年华的机会成本。

在监管方面，Elad 将1970年代安全游说团体扼杀核能的事件与 AI 监管进行类比："法国仍有70%的电力来自核能……事故在哪里？"他警告说，监管捕获和片面关注风险可能大幅减缓 AI 进步，而与此同时，AI 在医疗、教育和生产力方面的潜在收益是巨大的。
