### 2. Box CEO Aaron Levie — 企业 agent 的真实渗透率不到 1%

**EN:** Levie pushed back on the agent-hype curve with a number: "The vast majority of enterprise workflows have not actually had agents applied to them in any meaningful way. The economy probably has this deployed < 1% right now." At best, he says, companies run "a single pass of an LLM to make a decision or provide feedback, but very little real 'automation'." He splits the blame — partly enterprise adoption lag, but "the vast majority of it is really just due to the capability of AI itself." His turn signal: computer use, long-running agents, harness improvements and surrounding infrastructure have finally made complex workflows addressable. The end state he describes is org-level, not user-level: "This won't be about users prompting agents to complete a specific task for them, but instead the organization reengineering the underlying workflows." Agent swarms as a deployment primitive — choose an amount of compute, point it at a problem. Finance, life sciences, and healthcare are his named targets.

**中文：** Levie 用一个数字给 agent 的兴奋曲线降温："绝大多数企业工作流其实从未被 agent 以任何有意义的方式碰过。整个经济体的部署率现在大概不到 1%。"在他看来，企业最多也就是"让 LLM 跑一遍来做决策或给反馈，真正的'自动化'少得可怜"。他把原因拆成两半：一部分是企业采用技术的滞后，但"绝大部分原因其实就是 AI 自身的能力"。转折信号是：computer use、长时运行的 agent、harness 改进和周边基础设施，终于让复杂工作流变得可处理。他描述的终局是组织级的，不是用户级的："这不会是用户去提示 agent 完成某个具体任务，而是组织重新设计底层工作流。"agent swarm 成为部署原语——选定多少算力，对准一个问题。他点名的战场是金融、生命科学和医疗。
