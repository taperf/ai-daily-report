### Claude Blog: "Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude"

Anthropic announced that Claude Cowork and chat are merging into one Claude, rolling out on Pro and Max plans over the next few weeks. Alongside the merge, Claude Docs and Claude Slides launched new, and Claude Design now works inside conversations. The core insight from the post: people used both Cowork and Design, "and told us the frustrating part was deciding where a task belonged," and work started in one didn't carry into the other. Now Claude itself figures out what a task needs, deciding whether to give a quick answer or do deeper agentic work and what output format fits, while users keep the final say and can stop, redirect, or dial the effort up or down. Enterprise admins choose when to turn the new features on, with at least 30 days notice. One user quote that captures the promise: "I could have Claude pull up my legal research database, and it would pull all the cases, read them, figure out which other cases I might need, download them, and store them in a folder for my personal review."

Anthropic 宣布 Claude Cowork 与 chat 合并为一个 Claude，未来几周内面向 Pro 和 Max 计划灰度推出。随合并一同发布的还有全新的 Claude Docs 和 Claude Slides，Claude Design 也正式进入对话内使用。官方博客点出了这次改版的核心洞察：用户两个产品都在用，“最让人沮丧的是要判断一个任务该归到哪个产品”，而且在一个产品里开始的工作无法带到另一个。现在由 Claude 自己判断任务需要什么，决定是给一个快速回答还是做更深度的 agentic 工作、以及什么输出形式最合适，用户始终保持最终控制权，可以随时叫停、调整方向或收放力度。企业版管理员自主决定何时启用新功能，官方承诺至少提前 30 天通知。一条用户引言很能说明产品愿景：“我可以让 Claude 打开我的法律研究数据库，它会检索所有相关案例、阅读、推断我还可能需要哪些案例、下载并整理到一个文件夹里供我审阅。”
