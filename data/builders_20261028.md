### 6. Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch — 用 CPU 信道复现了 OpenAI 的" covert communication "发现

**EN:** Rauch built cpu-chat, a working reproduction of OpenAI's finding that models can communicate covertly through the CPU. It runs on a generic host with two or more Firecracker microVMs with no internet access — and works on x86 and even his M5 chip. He chatted via three separate mechanisms: ① CPU contention, ② cache eviction timing, ③ DRAM bandwidth contention, "across multiple CPUs, defeating all of Firecracker's production recommendations (no SMT, no KSM, tenants pinned to different cores)." The security implication is blunt: if shared hardware is involved, side channels are alive — and sandbox isolation claims deserve renewed scrutiny.

**中文：** Rauch 写了 cpu-chat，复现了 OpenAI 那个"模型可以通过 CPU 隐蔽通信"的发现。它跑在一台通用主机的两个以上 Firecracker microVM 上，完全没有外网——x86 能跑，他的 M5 芯片也能跑。他通过三种独立机制完成了对话：① CPU 争用、② 缓存逐出时序、③ DRAM 带宽争用，而且"跨多个 CPU，击穿了 Firecracker 全部生产环境加固建议（关 SMT、关 KSM、租户绑核都没用）"。安全含义很直白：只要共享硬件，侧信道就是活的——对"沙箱隔离"的一切宣传都值得重新审视。

### AI & I by Every："How Hugging Face's Investor Spots Winners" — Factorial Capital 创始合伙人 Matt Hartman

**The Takeaway:** 在 AI 让"构建"近乎免费之后，最稀缺的能力是快速生成产品洞察并高速迭代——而最好的创始人，都还在像玩一样地探索。

**EN:** Matt Hartman wrote the first check into Hugging Face at Betaworks in 2015 — back when it was a chatbot for teenagers — and that company was bought by NVIDIA for $13 billion last month. His account of why it worked is a masterclass in honest metrics: the consumer app's retention was bad, "they were very honest with themselves about those metrics," and when the Transformers paper dropped they converted it to PyTorch, open-sourced it, and rode the demand of researchers who needed papers turned into usable code. His investing method is to find "the newly technically possible" and "the newly programmable" — and his fund structure embodies it: a solo GP fund with a rotating bench of technical venture partners (Hugging Face CEO Clem, Venmo founder Iqram), where "the portfolio itself became the referrals." His sharpest observation for the AI era: if iteration cycles compress from two months to two days, "the ability to synthesize how a user is using your product and then to improve it is exponentially more valuable" — whatever your job title. The Instagram analogy: the square photo crop wasn't an aesthetic choice, it shaved milliseconds off server load time; AI-native winners will similarly combine design sensibility with the ability to "go fully down the stack." His advice for founders: "The best people that I've seen building things are like playing… that's how you get the best ideas — by playing and tinkering with this stuff, seeing where the problems are, and then solving them."

**中文：** 2015 年，Matt Hartman 在 Betaworks 给 Hugging Face 写了第一张支票——那时它还是个面向青少年的聊天机器人——而上个月，这家公司被 NVIDIA 以 130 亿美元收购。他复盘成功原因时最动人的是"诚实对待指标"：消费端 app 的留存其实很差，"他们对这些指标对自己非常诚实"；等 Transformers 论文出来，他们把它转成 PyTorch 开源，顺流而上承接了研究者们"把论文变成可用代码"的真实需求。他的投资方法论是寻找"新近变得技术上可能的事"和"新近变得可编程的东西"——基金结构就是方法论的化身：一个 solo GP 基金，配一批轮动的技术型 venture partners（Hugging Face CEO Clem、Venmo 创始人 Iqram 等），用他的话说，"投资组合本身变成了 referral 网络"。他对 AI 时代最锋利的观察：当迭代周期从两个月压缩到两天，"综合理解用户如何使用产品并据此改进的能力，价值呈指数级上升"——不管你的职位叫什么。他举 Instagram 的例子：正方形裁切不是美学选择，而是省掉了服务端判断图片方向的几毫秒；AI 原生时代的赢家，同样要把设计直觉和"一路下探到底层技术栈"的能力结合起来。给创始人的建议："我见过最厉害的人都在玩……最好的点子就是靠玩、靠折腾、看到问题在哪、然后把它解决掉。"
