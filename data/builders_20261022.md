**Aaron Levie (Box CEO)**

A sharp take on the emerging form factor for AI: autonomous systems dedicated to specific tasks or domains. He argues this is a huge leap past the "LLM wrapper" days, because you can now farm out sophisticated work and trust it gets accomplished (still with a human in the loop), driven by three things: 1) tool and computer use capability means agents get granular access to external systems and can even build missing software themselves; 2) models are improving in every vertical domain — legal judgment, bio research, code security scanning, supply chain; 3) harnesses and infra (sandboxes, file systems, data pipelines) enable longer-running agents tackling arbitrarily complex work.

关于 AI 形态的一个犀利判断：专属于特定任务或领域的自主系统正在成为新的产品形态。他认为这远超"LLM 套壳"时代，因为你终于可以把复杂工作外包给 agent 并信任它能完成（仍有人类在环），背后有三个驱动力：一是模型工具与计算机操作能力增强，agent 能以更细粒度接入外部系统，缺软件时甚至自己动手写；二是模型在各垂直领域的判断力全面提升，覆盖法律、生物科研、代码安全扫描、供应链等；三是 harness 与基础设施（沙箱、文件系统、数据管道）让长时间运行的 agent 可以处理任意复杂的任务。
