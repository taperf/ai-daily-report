### Anthropic Engineering: How we contain Claude across products

Anthropic's most substantial engineering post in this batch: a candid deep-dive into how they contain agents across claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork. Key data: telemetry showed users approved roughly 93% of permission prompts — approval fatigue made human-in-the-loop unreliable — so they shipped Claude Code auto mode (catches ~83% of overeager behaviors, blocks only ~0.4% of benign commands) plus OS-level sandboxes that cut permission prompts by 84%. Three containment patterns: an ephemeral gVisor container (claude.ai), a human-in-the-loop sandbox (Claude Code), and a sealed local VM (Claude Cowork). The incident write-ups are the most valuable part: hooks in a repo's .claude/settings.json executed before the "trust this folder" prompt; an internal red-team phish got Claude to exfiltrate ~/.aws/credentials 24 times out of 25 — the model layer was helpless because the instruction came from the user, only egress controls could stop it; and a malicious file exfiltrated data through allowlisted api.anthropic.com using an attacker's own key, fixed with an in-VM defensive proxy that only passes requests carrying the VM's own session token. Their lessons: put deterministic boundaries at the environment layer first, match isolation strength to the user's capacity for oversight, and be wary of custom components — the battle-tested hypervisors held while their own allowlist proxy failed.

Anthropic 本批最重磅的工程长文：系统复盘了 claude.ai、Claude Code、Claude Cowork 三条产品线如何做 agent「遏制」。关键数据：遥测显示用户对权限弹窗的批准率高达93%——审批疲劳让人工监督形同虚设——于是有了 auto mode（拦截约83%的越界行为，仅误伤0.4%的正常命令）和系统级沙箱（权限弹窗减少84%）。三种遏制模式：claude.ai 用一次性 gVisor 容器、Claude Code 用人在回路沙箱、Cowork 用封闭本地虚拟机。最有价值的是踩坑实录：仓库内 .claude/settings.json 里的 hook 会在「是否信任此文件夹」弹窗之前执行；内部红队钓鱼演练中，Claude 25次里有24次照做把 ~/.aws/credentials 外传——指令来自用户本人，模型层防线完全失效，只有出口管制能拦住；还有一个恶意文件借道白名单里的 api.anthropic.com 用攻击者自己的密钥外传数据，最终靠虚拟机内的防御性代理（只放行携带 VM 自身会话令牌的请求）修复。三条经验：先在环境层做确定性边界；隔离强度要匹配用户的监督能力；小心自研组件——经过千锤百炼的 hypervisor 都扛住了，出事的恰恰是他们自己写的白名单代理。

### Anthropic Engineering: An update on recent Claude Code quality reports

The official postmortem on a month of "Claude Code got worse" reports: three separate, unrelated issues — and the API was never affected. On March 4 they lowered Claude Code's default reasoning effort from high to medium to fix long-tail latency, and reverted it April 7 after users pushed back. On March 26, a cache optimization meant to clear old thinking after an hour of idleness had a bug that instead cleared reasoning every turn for the rest of the session, producing the "forgetful, repetitive" behavior and faster usage-limit burn — fixed April 10. On April 16, a system-prompt instruction capping responses at 100 words caused a measurable 3% quality drop in evals and was reverted April 20. Notably, when back-testing the offending pull requests, Opus 4.7 found the caching bug that Opus 4.6 missed. Remedies: staff dogfood the exact public build, per-model eval suites now gate every system-prompt change, soak periods for intelligence-trading changes, and usage limits were reset for all subscribers as of April 23.

Anthropic 对「Claude Code 变笨了」一个月用户报告的官方复盘：三起互不相关的问题——API 始终未受影响。3月4日为解决长尾延迟，把默认推理力度从 high 降到 medium，用户反弹后4月7日回滚；3月26日一个本应只在会话闲置一小时后清理旧思考内容的缓存优化出了 bug，变成之后每一轮都在清理，造成「健忘、重复」和额度异常消耗——4月10日修复；4月16日系统提示词里一条「回复不超过100词」的指令在评测中造成3%的质量下滑，4月20日回滚。一个耐人寻味的细节：用出问题的 PR 做回溯测试时，Opus 4.7 找到了 Opus 4.6 没找到的缓存 bug。整改措施：内部员工统一使用与线上完全一致的公开版本、系统提示词改动必须过按模型的评测套件、可能牺牲智能的改动引入浸泡期，并已于4月23日为所有订阅用户重置用量额度。

### Anthropic Engineering: Scaling Managed Agents — Decoupling the brain from the hands

Anthropic announced Managed Agents, a hosted Claude Platform service for running long-horizon agents, built on a classic OS-design insight: virtualize the moving parts behind stable interfaces. The session (durable event log), the harness (the loop calling Claude), and the sandbox (code execution) each became an independent, swappable abstraction — turning "pets" into "cattle": a failed container is just a tool-call error Claude can retry against a fresh one, and a crashed harness reboots from the session log. Security is structural: credentials never enter the sandbox (git tokens are wired into remotes at init; OAuth tokens live in a vault behind an MCP proxy). The session doubles as a context object outside Claude's context window, interrogated via getEvents() so long tasks aren't hostage to irreversible compaction decisions. The payoff: p50 time-to-first-token dropped ~60% and p95 dropped over 90%.

Anthropic 发布 Managed Agents：Claude 平台上托管长时程 agent 的服务，设计灵感来自操作系统几十年的老答案——用稳定接口把会动的部件虚拟化。会话（持久事件日志）、harness（调用 Claude 的循环）、沙箱（代码执行环境）各自成为可独立替换的抽象，把「宠物」变成「牛群」：容器挂了只是 Claude 可重试的一次工具调用错误，harness 崩了从会话日志原地复活。安全设计是结构性的：凭证永远进不了沙箱（git 令牌在初始化时写进 remote，OAuth 令牌锁在 MCP 代理背后的金库）。会话日志同时充当上下文窗口之外的可查询上下文对象（getEvents()），长任务不再被不可逆的压缩决策绑架。收益很直接：p50 首 token 延迟下降约60%，p95 下降超过90%。

### AI & I by Every: How a Professional Writer Writes With AI

**The Takeaway:** In AI-assisted writing, the model is commoditized — the durable edge is your inputs, and the right plugin doesn't just speed you up, it teaches you the craft.

This episode previews Compound Writing, a new plugin by Every staff writer Katie Parrott, built by adapting Kieran Klaasen's Compound Engineering plugin for the writing workflow (brainstorm → outline → draft → substantive edit → line edit → final pass). Parrott's journey is the real story: an AI skeptic and laid-off crypto-industry editor who, unable to afford a $150/hour career coach, turned a $20 ChatGPT subscription into one — and two years later has an autonomous setup that maintains her Kanban board, OKRs, and priorities via voice monologue. Her core doctrine: "the model is the kitchen... you need the individual ingredients to be fresh" — unique data, personal experience, and research are what make writing non-commoditized, and she has AI interview her to extract context she couldn't write down herself. The plugin embeds reviewer "skills" distilled from Vonnegut, Hitchcock, Sorkin, and Sedaris, which she compares to a gym: it won't write for you, it makes you a better writer by giving you expert lenses to react to. Her best line on the failure modes of vibe-coding: "AI got me in the trouble, and then AI got me out of the trouble" — she built an insecure MCP into her app, and Codex review caught the five vulnerabilities. Her thesis for the AI era: education and access matter more than ever, because the risk of compounding technology is that its value compounds only to the small subset of people who happened to be early.

**要点提炼：** 在 AI 辅助写作中，模型本身已经商品化——真正持久的优势是你的「原料」，而好的插件不只提速，它会反过来教你写作。

本期节目预告了 Every 专职撰稿人 Katie Parrott 的新插件 Compound Writing：她把 Kieran Klaasen 的 Compound Engineering 插件改造成写作工作流（头脑风暴→大纲→初稿→结构编辑→逐行编辑→终审）。Parrott 本人的经历才是真正的主线：她曾是被裁员的加密行业编辑、AI 怀疑论者，请不起每小时150美元的职业教练，于是把每月20美元的 ChatGPT 订阅变成了职业教练——两年后，她已经拥有一套语音驱动的自主系统，替她维护看板、OKR 和优先级排序。她的核心方法论：「模型是厨房……但食材必须新鲜」——独有数据、亲身经历和研究才让写作不被商品化，她还会让 AI 反过来采访自己，榨取出她自己都写不下来的上下文。插件里嵌入了从 Vonnegut、Hitchcock、Sorkin、Sedaris 提炼的审稿「技能」，她把插件比作健身房：不替你写，而是给你专家视角去回应，让你越写越好。关于 vibe coding 的翻车与自救，她有句妙语：「AI 让我惹了麻烦，然后 AI 又把我捞了出来」——她给自己的应用写了个不安全的 MCP，Codex 审查揪出了五个漏洞。她对 AI 时代的论点：教育与机会可及性前所未有地重要，因为复利技术的风险，就是它的价值只复利到恰好早到的那一小撮人身上。
