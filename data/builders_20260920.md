### 7. No Priors：扩散模型之父 Stefano Ermon 谈「为什么扩散模型会赢下 AI 推理」

**The Takeaway:** The next phase of AI economics will be decided at inference time, not in training, and diffusion's parallel generation gives it a structural cost and speed advantage that autoregressive models cannot match.

Stefano Ermon is a longtime Stanford professor and the co-founder and CEO of Inception, and he is one of the fathers of diffusion: the score-based generative models he developed with Yang Song in 2019 became the technology underlying modern image, video, and music generation. Two years ago he turned the same machinery toward text. In 2024 his lab published a landmark result: a diffusion language model matched an autoregressive model in quality at GPT-2 scale while generating text 10x faster. That paper became his company. Inception is now about 50 people, its Mercury models are in production and benchmark-comparable to speed-optimized frontier models, and the team had to build its own serving engine from scratch because diffusion LLMs cannot run on vLLM or SGLang.

His core argument is an architectural one. Transformers won in 2017 because they parallelized training, but autoregressive inference is still sequential and memory-bound, generating one token at a time in a way that maps poorly to GPUs. Diffusion inference, by contrast, processes many tokens simultaneously, which makes the inference workload resemble the training workload that GPUs excel at. As he put it: "The bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win." The proof point is a voice-agent customer, Open Call, that previously served its LLM on Cerebras custom chips and switched to Mercury on standard NVIDIA GPUs, getting comparable speed with better availability and lower cost.

He also flagged a less obvious advantage: diffusion models are easier to control. Because generation is coarse-to-fine, you can steer an output toward constraints or reward functions mid-generation, instead of waiting until the full object is complete to score it, something he believes will enable product experiences autoregressive models cannot offer. His estimate, based on OpenRouter's task taxonomy, is that 20 to 30 percent of workloads are latency-critical, a conservative floor for addressable demand. And on whether the bet will pay beyond speed, he is honest: "We don't know what we're going to find," though academic evidence already suggests diffusion models are more data-efficient, since denoising acts as built-in data augmentation.

**核心结论：** AI 经济学的下一阶段将在推理端决出胜负，而非训练端。扩散模型的并行生成在成本和速度上具备自回归模型无法企及的结构性优势。

Stefano Ermon 是斯坦福资深教授、Inception 联合创始人兼 CEO，也是「扩散模型之父」之一：他与 Yang Song 在 2019 年开发的 score-based 生成模型，构成了今天图像、视频和音乐生成技术的底层。两年前，他把同一套方法转向了文本。2024 年他的实验室发表了一个里程碑结果：扩散语言模型在 GPT-2 规模上追平了自回归模型的质量，同时文本生成速度快 10 倍。这篇论文直接催生了 Inception。公司现在约 50 人，Mercury 系列模型已投入生产环境、跑分对标前沿实验室的速度优化模型；由于扩散 LLM 无法在 vLLM 或 SGLang 上运行，团队不得不从零自建推理引擎。

他的核心论点是架构层面的：Transformer 在 2017 年胜出靠的是训练并行化，但自回归模型的推理依然是串行且受内存带宽限制的，逐个 token 生成的方式与 GPU 的特性严重不匹配。扩散模型的推理则同时处理大量 token，使推理负载变得和训练负载一样「对 GPU 友好」。用他的原话说：「Bitter lesson 的推论是，更并行的方案终将获胜。」实证来自语音 agent 客户 Open Call：它原先在 Cerebras 定制芯片上跑 LLM，后来切换到标准 NVIDIA GPU 上的 Mercury，速度相当，可用性更好、成本更低。

他还指出了一个不那么显眼的优势：扩散模型更易控制。由于生成是「从粗到细」的过程，可以在生成中途就把输出导向约束条件或奖励函数，而不必等整个对象生成完毕再打分。他认为这将催生自回归模型给不出的产品体验。基于 OpenRouter 的任务分类，他估算有 20% 到 30% 的工作负载对延迟敏感，这只是可触达需求的下限。至于押注扩散能否在速度之外带来更多回报，他很坦诚：「我们不知道会发现什么。」不过学术界已有证据表明扩散模型的数据效率更高，因为去噪过程本身就是内建的数据增强。
