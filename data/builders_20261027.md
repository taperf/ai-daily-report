### 2. Box CEO Aaron Levie

**EN:** Levie assessed the on-the-ground security reality of large enterprises: the fragmented risk surface "is only going to get more gnarly" — endless libraries, sprawling infrastructure, and highly fragmented data mean most organizations don't even know their risk state at any given time; some systems grant privileges too broadly, others run on security-through-obscurity. His thesis: once you get data and infra into a better spot, agent swarms can fan out, leverage any security tool, test attack permutations, read logs, and process enterprise data for risk — a capability that only emerged in the past few months with long-running agents and computer use. That's a huge opportunity across every layer of the security stack, and one reason to be optimistic about the offense/defense balance. "But everyone does need to do the hard work of getting the basics right at the start."

**中文：** Levie 评估了大型企业安全的地面实况：碎片化的风险面"只会越来越棘手"——无休止的依赖库、 sprawling 的基础设施、高度碎片化的数据，意味着大多数组织在任何时刻都不清楚自己的风险状态；有些系统权限开得太宽，有些则靠"隐蔽即安全"混日子。他的论点是：先把数据和基础设施治理到位，然后 agent 集群就能铺开——调用任何安全工具、测试各种攻击变体、读日志、评估企业数据风险。这种能力是过去几个月才出现的，前提是 long-running agent 和 computer use 足够成熟。这是安全栈每一层（身份、防火墙、网络、代码扫描、数据保护）的巨大机会，也是对攻防平衡保持乐观的至少一个理由。"但每个人还是得先把基本功做扎实。"

### No Priors: "The Changing Physics of Cybersecurity and Drug Development" — OpenAI 总裁 Greg Brockman（10月15日上线）

**The Takeaway:** Brockman 断言 AI 已经"无法否认地"能产生新知识，而网络安全和药物研发的"物理学"正在因此改变——安全支出最终应大幅超过软件支出。

**EN:** OpenAI President Greg Brockman made his strongest claims yet on machine-generated knowledge. OpenAI published solutions to almost 400 important open math problems, and he stressed the proofs contain "beautiful insights" and surprising cross-domain moves — not incomprehensible machine-checked cases like the four-color theorem. "At this point, it's just impossible to deny — it is definitively, yes, possible" that AI creates new knowledge. On why math fell first: data. "Compute, data, algorithms equals capability" — and biology's data bottleneck, plus regulatory filings that take a year to write, are the real constraints on AI drug discovery; N-of-1 diseases where the cure already sits in some paper are a near-term win. On security, he declared a "defender's window": peak frontier AI can help defenders before offensive capabilities fully diffuse. OpenAI committed $1 billion to get defenders access through its Daybreak program, and internally pulled 25 production engineers off projects to run their best model against their own infrastructure until vulnerabilities saturated. His boldest line: "Cyber spend, security spend should actually vastly outstrip software spend" — because when software is nearly free, reliability is the product. On products: DOTS, an always-on proactive agent ("my Dots notices things I never even asked it to notice"), best UI is no UI, and with GPT-6 shipped to over a billion users plus 1.2B consumers alongside a leading enterprise/coding business, consumer and enterprise AI are converging. His closing frame, via Steve Jobs' iMac handle: humans must feel they're in the driver's seat.

**中文：** OpenAI 总裁 Greg Brockman 发表了关于"机器产生新知识"的最强表态。OpenAI 公布了近 400 个重要公开数学问题的解答，他强调这些证明里有"漂亮的洞察"和出人意料的跨领域手法——不是四色定理那种没法读的机器穷举。"AI 能否产生新知识这个问题，现在已经无法否认——答案是明确的、肯定的。"至于为什么数学先被攻下：数据。"算力、数据、算法等于能力"——而生物学的数据瓶颈，加上要写一年的注册申报文件，才是 AI 药物研发的真正约束；那些特效药其实已经躺在某篇论文里的 N-of-1 罕见病，是最近的速赢点。关于安全，他提出了"防御者窗口"的概念：在攻击能力全面扩散之前，最前沿的 AI 能力先帮到防御者。OpenAI 已承诺 10 亿美元，通过 Daybreak 项目让防御者用上其最强模型；内部则抽调 25 名生产工程师暂停项目，用当时最强的模型攻击自家基础设施，直到该模型能找到的漏洞全部饱和。他最大胆的一句话："安全支出最终应该大幅超过软件支出"——因为当软件几乎免费时，可靠性才是产品本身。谈产品：DOTS 是常驻的主动式 agent（"我的 Dots 会注意到我从没让它注意的事"）；最好的 UI 是没有 UI；GPT-6 已推给超过 10 亿用户，加上 12 亿消费者和领先的企业/编程业务，消费级与企业级 AI 正在合流。结尾他借乔布斯给 iMac 装把手的故事点题：人类必须始终感觉自己握着方向盘。
