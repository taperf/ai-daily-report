**Training Data — "Making Cities Awesome: Peregrine's Nick Noone & Ben Rudolph"**

Nick Noone (ex-Palantir SOCOM) and Ben Rudolph (ex-UN Refugee Agency) discuss building Peregrine, an AI platform for public safety that explicitly rejects the surveillance-state model. Their core thesis: the way to make cities awesome starts with safety — both objective and perceived — and technology should preserve individual privacy while helping institutions do better work with the data they already have.

The most striking idea is Peregrine's "anti network effects" business model. Unlike data-collection companies, Peregrine doesn't bring new data to customers — it helps them use what they already own, with strict data governance, permission controls, and local sovereignty. Noone explains: "We're fundamentally not in any shape or form in the business of bringing more data to the customer that they don't already have."

On long-horizon agents, the team revealed that 90% of their Python notebook integrations are now written by agents running for hours, analyzing databases and ontology, spawning sub-agents that report back to an orchestrator. They've also built a "cold case agent" that processes 200-300GB of evidence (video, audio, images, PDFs) to help detectives — recently helping a Wisconsin county place a suspect at both the crime scene and where the body was found, based on scattered cell records within massive datasets.

On forward deployed engineering, Noone frames it as "R&D and growth" — not a cost center. The team has discovered product primitives by watching deployment strategists hack around limitations in customer environments, from comment-based property editing to hurricane simulators built by someone who'd been at the company for a month.

Noone 和 Rudolph 讨论了他们创建的 Peregrine——一个明确拒绝监控国家模式的公共安全 AI 平台。他们的核心论点：让城市变得更美好的起点是安全——包括客观安全和感知安全——技术应该在保护个人隐私的同时，帮助机构更好地使用已有的数据。

最引人注目的理念是 Peregrine 的"反网络效应"商业模式。与数据收集公司不同，Peregrine 不会给客户带来新数据——它帮助他们使用已有的数据，并通过严格的数据治理、权限控制和本地主权来保护数据。Noone 说："我们从根本上说，完全不是在做给客户带来他们没有的数据这个生意。"

在长期 agent 方面，团队透露他们现在90%的 Python notebook 集成代码都由 agent 编写——这些 agent 运行数小时，分析数据库和本体论，生成子 agent 向协调器汇报。他们还构建了一个"冷案 agent"，处理200-300GB的证据（视频、音频、图片、PDF）来帮助侦探——最近在威斯康星州一个县帮助定位了嫌疑人，基于海量数据中散落的手机记录，确定了嫌疑人在犯罪现场和尸体发现地的位置。

关于前线部署工程，Noone 将其定位为"研发与增长"——而非成本中心。团队通过观察部署策略师在客户环境中绕过限制的方式发现了产品原语，从基于评论的属性编辑到入职仅一个月就有人构建的飓风模拟器。
