### 6. Aaron Levie (Box CEO) — 知识工作的终局：从「做事」到「给 Agent 定目标」

Levie's window-into-the-future thesis: coding shows what will happen to most knowledge work. Step one — humans shift from doing research/typing/value creation manually to coordinating the work agents do. Step two — as coordination itself becomes the bottleneck, we give agents goals to optimize and they solve ever-larger problems. A large percentage of knowledge work may converge with this form factor because the productivity gains are simply too large: agents will use computers, leverage existing tools, and write new code on the fly.

> Box CEO Aaron Levie 的预判：编程是知识工作的未来预演。第一步，人类从「亲自调研、打字、创造价值」转向「协调 Agent 干活」；第二步，当协调本身也拖慢 Agent，人类就只负责给目标，Agent 自主解决越来越大的问题。由于生产力增益太大，大部分知识工作最终都会向这个形态收敛——Agent 会自己操作电脑、调用现有工具、随手写出新代码。

### Training Data (Sequoia): OpenAI's Noam Brown — Why Game Theory Didn't Lead to General Intelligence (Live at No Limit Finals)

🔗 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRaFbDFBl_A · published 2026-11-24

**The Takeaway:** Game theory assumes everyone is rational — people aren't — and that single flawed assumption is why the road to general intelligence ran through modeling human behavior and RL-trained reasoning, not elegant equilibrium math.

> **核心结论：** 博弈论的整个前提是「所有玩家都是理性的」——但人不是。正是这个错误假设，让通往通用智能的路径最终绕开了优雅的均衡数学，转向了对人类行为建模 + 强化学习训练的推理能力。

**From poker to diplomacy to reasoning.** Noam Brown (OpenAI reasoning research; previously created Libratus and Cicero) started in grad school building poker bots because a perfect strategy existed but nobody knew it — "if you could find it, you could just make unlimited money." Poker taught him imperfect-information techniques, but the real ambition was Diplomacy: a natural-language game of negotiation and alliance where game theory itself failed him. The ultimatum game is his go-to example — theory says offer a penny; in reality, people reject the offer out of spite. Bots trained on rationality assumptions exhibited exactly these irrational behaviors, and the winning approach was counterintuitive: build imitation models of human irrationality, train against them, "assume people are gonna behave like people."

> **从扑克到外交到推理。** Noam Brown（OpenAI 推理研究负责人，曾打造 Libratus 与 Cicero）读博时做扑克 AI 的动机很朴素——完美策略存在但没人知道，「找到了就能赚无限的钱」。扑克教会他不完全信息博弈，但真正让他栽跟头的是外交游戏（Diplomacy）：最后通牒博弈里理论最优解是只给对方一分钱，现实中对方会因为愤怒直接掀桌。假设理性的机器人恰恰会做出这种不近人情的举动，而最终有效的做法很反直觉——先训练模型精确模仿人类的非理性，再让 AI 与这些「模仿人类」对练。

**The 2022 crisis of faith — and the bet on reasoning.** When it became clear game theory wasn't the path, Brown faced a choice between his lifelong expertise and what the evidence supported. He chose to pivot to general reasoning, joining OpenAI partly because everywhere else told him "just pretrain a bigger model." His counterargument: making GPT-4 competent at Go would take about six orders of magnitude more training. The key unlock was RL on chain of thought — and the observation that the longer a model thinks before responding, the better it performs. That became the way to scale test-time compute generally, which became o1.

> **2022 年的信仰危机，以及押注推理。** 当博弈论路线被证伪，Brown 在毕生专长与证据之间选择了后者，转向通用推理并加入 OpenAI—— partly 因为其他公司都跟他说「把预训练规模再加大就行了」，而他算过：要让 GPT-4 学会下围棋，大约需要再大六个数量级的训练量。真正的突破是对思维链做强化学习，以及那个关键观察：模型回答前思考得越久，表现越好。这条路线最终成为 o1。

**Jagged intelligence, and the Centaur period.** Brown's personal benchmark for every new model: ask it to build a poker bot. Opus 5.5/5.6 was the first to "10x–20x" his output without being able to finish alone; even Astra can one-shot an excellent river solver but not a full bot. What's missing: long-horizon planning and knowing when to step back — models will spend hours perfecting CFR numerics that don't matter. Right now models are complements to human ability, and "we're entering a Centaur period" where human+AI beats either alone. On RSI: all of technological progress is one giant RSI loop; LLMs have been used to curate their own pretraining data for years. His most controversial take: "if somebody is a promising student, the best thing to do is actually to drop out and join the labs now." On AI vs. humans at the WSOP in five years: about a 20% chance, because variance and all-in luck remain.

> **参差智能与「半人马时代」。** Brown 每出一个新模型都要它做扑克机器人：Opus 5.5/5.6 是第一次让他效率提升 10–20 倍但仍无法独立完成的模型；连 Astra 也能单指令写出优秀的河牌 solver，却做不出完整机器人。缺的是长程规划——模型会花几小时去打磨根本不重要的 CFR 数值精度。当下模型是人类能力的互补，「我们正在进入半人马时代」，人+AI 强于任何单方。关于 RSI：整个技术文明本来就是一场巨型递归自我改进；LLM 早已被用来清洗自己的预训练数据。他最具争议的观点：「有前途的学生现在最好的选择是退学加入实验室。」AI 五年内能否在 WSOP 击败人类冠军？「大概 20% 概率——扑克的方差和运气还在。」
