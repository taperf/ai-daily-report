### 4. Guillermo Rauch（Vercel CEO）

His most substantive post of the week: every successful agent (Muse, Instinct, OpenClaw, Claude Code) has three components — 🧠 Brain (model + harness), 👐 Hands (tools/computer/browser), 🗃️ Files (memories/skills/repos). Running it all on one stateful Mac Mini is the "easy way"; cost-efficient cloud agents require decoupling each part. Vercel shipped Sandbox for the compute, and today launches **Drives** to decouple storage — e.g. a nightly memory-consolidation cron job ("dreaming") can read/write the agent's files without booting the full computer. Also: he's neurotic about shell startup time, and Opus 5.5 found shell optimizations other models missed — "anytime you open a new terminal, you might be silently suffering."

本周信息量最大的一条：所有成功的 agent（Muse、Instinct、OpenClaw、Claude Code）都有三个组件——🧠 大脑（模型+harness）、👐 双手（工具/电脑/浏览器）、🗃️ 文件（记忆/技能/仓库）。全塞进一台有状态的 Mac Mini 是"简单做法"；要在云端高性价比跑 agent，就必须把三部分解耦。Vercel 已为算力发布 Sandbox，今天推出 **Drives** 解耦存储——比如每晚跑一个记忆整理定时任务（"做梦"），可以直接读写 agent 文件而无需启动完整电脑。另外：他对 shell 启动速度有执念，Opus 5.5 找到了其他模型漏掉的优化——"每次打开新终端，你可能都在默默受苦。"

### 1. How we contain Claude across products

Twelve months ago Anthropic would have rejected out of hand the idea of granting Claude access sufficient to take down an internal service; today that level of access is routine and developers are more productive for it. The post frames agent risk as two components — failure likelihood (steadily driven down by safeguards and training) and blast radius (only grows with capability and access) — and argues that as agents do work that once required a person or a team, the cost of *not* deploying grows large enough that the risk-reward calculus changes.

十二个月前，Anthropic 会断然拒绝授予 Claude 足以搞挂一个内部服务的权限；今天这种权限已是常规操作，开发者也因此效率更高。文章把 agent 风险拆成两个变量——故障概率（随安全护栏和模型训练持续下降）和爆炸半径（随能力和权限扩大而增长）——并指出当 agent 能完成原本需要一个甚至一个团队才能完成的工作时，"不部署"的成本已经大到足以改变风险收益的计算。

🔗 https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

### 2. Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands

On designing harnesses for long-running agent work: harnesses encode assumptions about what Claude can't do on its own, and those assumptions go stale as models improve — e.g. Sonnet 4.5's "context anxiety" (wrapping up prematurely as it sensed its context limit), fixed by adding context resets to the harness. Pairs nicely with Rauch's brain/hands/files thesis above.

关于如何为长时运行的 agent 设计 harness：harness 里编码着"Claude 自己做不了什么"的假设，而这些假设会随模型进步而过时——例如 Sonnet 4.5 的"上下文焦虑"（感觉到上下文快满了就提前收工），解决方法是给 harness 加上下文重置。与上面 Rauch 的大脑/双手/文件论正好互相印证。

🔗 https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents
