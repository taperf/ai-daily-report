### 2. Aaron Levie（Box CEO）：Agent 落地呈双峰分布，"现在看到的只是 1% 的未来"

Box CEO Aaron Levie published a detailed analysis of where agentic AI actually stands: adoption is "very bimodal" — coding and coding-adjacent tasks have taken off, everything else lags far behind. Even within coding, only a small percentage of developers run background agents on parallel projects while the rest still work 1:1. His diagnosis for why knowledge work lags: most workflows "still need to be reengineered" — data rewiring, new accountability and liability practices, governance and compliance paradigm changes. His conclusion: "you can basically expect 100X more agent adoption from what we've seen so far."

中文翻译：Box CEO Aaron Levie 发布了一段关于 agent AI 真实落地状态的详细分析：落地呈"非常双峰"的分布——编程及编程相关任务已经起飞，其余一切远远落后。即便在编程领域，也只是一小部分开发者在用后台 agent 并行推进项目，其余人仍在与 agent 一对一协作。他对知识型工作滞后的诊断是：大多数工作流"仍需重新工程化"——数据要重新接线，要建立针对 agent 参与决策的问责与责任归属新规则，治理与合规范式也要变。他的结论是："基本上可以预期，agent 的落地规模将达到目前水平的 100 倍。"

### 6. Ryo Lu（设计师，曾操刀 Cursor / Notion / Stripe）："均值收敛"长文——AI 时代最稀缺的是"有人味"的作品

Ryo Lu, the designer behind Cursor, Notion and Stripe's early interfaces, published the day's longest and most thoughtful essay: "convergence to the mean." His argument: when everyone looks at everyone else to decide what to make, "we eventually stop making anything of our own" — we copy what works, and "eventually the feedback stops informing the work and starts authoring it." AI makes this loop frictionless: it can draw from more references than a person could see in a lifetime, but "access to every perspective isn't the same as having one." His call: approach making things more like artists — software itself can be a medium that "expresses beliefs about how life should feel." The kicker: "What is the point of everyone being able to make something, if we all end up making the same thing?"

中文翻译：曾为 Cursor、Notion、Stripe 设计界面的 Ryo Lu 发了当天最长也最有思想密度的一篇长文：《均值收敛》。核心论点：当所有人都盯着别人决定自己做什么时，"我们最终会不再做出任何属于自己的东西"——我们复制有效的东西，"到最后，反馈不再为作品提供信息，而是开始替你写作品"。AI 让这个循环变得毫无摩擦：它能调用的参考比一个人一辈子看过的都多，但"接触所有视角不等于拥有一个视角"。他的主张：像艺术家一样去做东西——软件本身可以是一种媒介，"表达你对生活应该是什么手感的一种信念"。结尾一击："如果每个人都能做出东西，但最后做出来的都一模一样，那'人人都能创造'的意义何在？"
