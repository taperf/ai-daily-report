### No Priors：Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong 谈 Agentic Finance

**The Takeaway:** AI agents will need their own financial infrastructure, and Armstrong is betting Coinbase becomes the bank for the agentic economy.

**Brian Armstrong** is the co-founder and CEO of Coinbase, one of the world's earliest and most important crypto companies, and co-founder of longevity startup New Limit. His current obsession: agentic finance.

**On why payments rails break for agents:** Armstrong walked through the math of traditional card fees: about 30¢ flat plus a percentage per transaction. That structure works for humans buying coffee, but "arguably, it doesn't really work well for anything under a dollar," and the data is striking: "about 76% of the agent ecommerce transactions we're seeing are under 30¢." In other words, most of the emerging agentic economy is invisible to the existing payment system, not because agents can't pay, but because the fees eat the transaction alive.

**On banking the AIs:** His framing is deliberately provocative: "We don't want the AIs to be unbanked." Coinbase has built simple tools where a single prompt, pasted into your AI agent, gives it its own financial account. The philosophical shift here is real: financial identity for non-humans is becoming a product category.

**On the size of the prize:** Armstrong's long-view prediction is the boldest part: "There'll be more agents than humans in the not too distant future," and the agentic economy "will be bigger than the human economy at some point." Whether or not you buy the timeline, the strategic logic is coherent: whoever provides the account, payment, and settlement layer for agents inherits a market that scales with agent count rather than human count.

**核心要点：** AI agent 需要自己的金融基础设施，而 Armstrong 押注 Coinbase 成为 agent 经济的银行。

**Brian Armstrong** 是 Coinbase 联合创始人兼 CEO，全球最早也最重要的加密公司之一，同时是抗衰老公司 New Limit 的联合创始人。他当下的关注焦点：agentic finance（agent 金融）。

**为什么现有支付体系对 agent 失效：** Armstrong 算了一笔账：传统银行卡手续费约为每笔 30¢ 固定费加百分比。这套结构对人类买咖啡没问题，但"对 1 美元以下的交易基本不成立"，而数据很惊人："我们看到的 agent 电商交易中约 76% 低于 30¢。"换言之，新兴的 agent 经济大部分在现有支付体系里是隐形的：不是 agent 付不起，而是手续费直接吃掉整笔交易。

**给 AI 开银行账户：** 他的表述刻意挑衅："我们不能让 AI 没有银行账户（unbanked）。" Coinbase 已做了简单的工具：一条 prompt 粘贴进你的 AI agent，它就能拥有自己的金融账户。这里的观念转变是实质性的：非人类的金融身份正在成为一个产品品类。

**市场有多大：** 最大胆的是他的长期预判："在不远的将来，agent 的数量会超过人类"，而 agent 经济"终将在某个时点超过人类经济"。无论你是否认同这个时间表，战略逻辑是自洽的：谁为 agent 提供账户、支付和结算层，谁就继承了一个随 agent 数量而非人类数量增长的市场。
