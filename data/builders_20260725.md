### Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

Levie highlighted a post from Anthropic's Head of Economics finding that jobs have been less negatively impacted by AI than feared. The key insight: AI is "skill-biased and labor-augmenting" — it complements domain expertise rather than replacing it. Most jobs can't be fully automated, only certain tasks. When you automate specific tasks, you often get more output from those jobs, maintaining or even raising demand. Levie invokes Jevons paradox: as AI makes work more efficient, demand for that work can actually increase.

Levie 重点分享了 Anthropic 经济学负责人的研究发现：AI 对工作的负面影响比担心的要小。核心洞察是：AI 是"技能偏向且劳动增强的"——它补充了领域专业知识，而非替代它。大多数工作无法被完全自动化，只有某些任务可以。当你自动化特定任务时，这些工作的产出往往会增加，从而维持甚至提高需求。Levie 援引了杰文斯悖论：随着 AI 提高工作效率，对该工作的需求实际上可能增加。

### AI & I by Every — How Every's Team Used AI to Ship Its Biggest Launch Ever

**核心要点：** 工作的未来不是用 AI 替代人类，而是让 AI 处理执行，让人类专注于真正推动进展的创造性、战略性决策。

Every CEO Dan Shipper and his team — Yash (product/growth), Austin (growth), and Douglas (creative director) — shared the behind-the-scenes story of how they built and launched "All Access," a $625 annual plan featuring the "Builder Pack" (discounts and credits to 10+ AI tools). The launch generated the largest subscription revenue increase in Every's history.

Every CEO Dan Shipper 和他的团队——Yash（产品/增长）、Austin（增长）和 Douglas（创意总监）——分享了他们如何构建和发布"All Access"的幕后故事，这是一个 625 美元的年费计划，包含"Builder Pack"（10 多个 AI 工具的折扣和积分）。这次发布创造了 Every 历史上最大的订阅收入增长。

The most striking story came from Austin, who described how he used Codex (with the team's "LFG" compound engineering harness) to automate an entire email marketing campaign. He took a screenshot of a Slack conversation about targeting users who showed checkout intent but didn't convert, dropped it into Codex with a simple prompt — "can you do this?" — and went to the gym. When he returned, Codex had created four audience cohorts, drafted personalized emails for each, run them through their AI writing tool Spiral for tone consistency, pulled a high-performing social share image from past campaigns, and scheduled everything for the next morning. The emails alone drove over $25,000 in revenue within hours.

最引人注目的故事来自 Austin，他描述了如何使用 Codex（配合团队的 "LFG" compound engineering harness）来自动化整个电子邮件营销活动。他截取了一段关于 targeting 有结账意向但未转化的用户的 Slack 对话截图，用简单的提示词"你能做这个吗？"丢给 Codex，然后去健身房。回来时，Codex 已经创建了四个受众 cohorts，为每个 cohort 起草了个性化邮件，通过他们的 AI 写作工具 Spiral 保持了语气一致性，从过去的 campaign 中提取了高表现的社交分享图片，并将一切安排在了第二天早上。仅这些邮件就在几小时内产生了超过 25,000 美元的收入。
