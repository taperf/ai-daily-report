### AI & I by Every — "Why the Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social"

**The Takeaway:** The next paradigm-shifting AI consumer product won't come from a research lab — it will come from a product genius who understands social dynamics and network effects, just as Pinterest, Snap, and Instagram emerged once web infrastructure matured beyond Google's technical-first era.

**核心结论：** 下一个颠覆性的 AI 消费级产品不会来自研究实验室——它将来自一位理解社交动态和网络效应的产品天才，就像 Pinterest、Snap 和 Instagram 在网络基础设施超越 Google 的技术优先时代后崛起一样。

Benchmark partner Sarah Tavel (ex-Pinterest) joined Dan Shipper to map consumer technology cycles onto the current AI wave. She observed that every major platform shift follows a pattern: the founding team is deeply technical at first (Google = 95% backend), then gradually shifts toward product intuition as infrastructure matures (Pinterest, Snap, Instagram CEOs "weren't technical at all — they were product geniuses"). ChatGPT and Character AI, she argues, are still in the "technical founding team" phase — Noam Shazeer saw the product as the model. The real opportunity, Tavel believes, is in multiplayer experiences with network effects and status-seeking dynamics. She predicts someone will build a UGC community for AI prompts and workflows, where power users make it "so much easier for the rest of us." She also notes that most people use ChatGPT like Google — barely scratching the surface — and democratizing the power-user experience requires new interfaces, not just better models.

Benchmark 合伙人 Sarah Tavel（前 Pinterest）与 Dan Shipper 对谈，将消费技术周期映射到当前的 AI 浪潮。她观察到每个重大平台变革都遵循一个模式：创始团队最初深度技术导向（Google = 95% 后端），然后随着基础设施成熟逐渐转向产品直觉（Pinterest、Snap、Instagram 的 CEO"完全不技术——他们是产品天才"）。她认为 ChatGPT 和 Character AI 仍处于"技术创始团队"阶段——Noam Shazeer 将产品等同于模型。Tavel 相信，真正的机会在于具有网络效应和身份追求动态的多人体验。她预测有人会为 AI prompt 和工作流构建一个 UGC 社区，让高级用户"让我们其他人轻松得多"。她还指出，大多数人像用 Google 一样用 ChatGPT——几乎只触碰到表面——而民主化高级用户体验需要新的界面，而不仅仅是更好的模型。
