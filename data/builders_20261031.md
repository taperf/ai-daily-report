### 2. Replit CEO Amjad Masad — 开启 autofix 之后，软件开始自己维护自己

**EN:** Masad made three moves that add up to a thesis about where app-building goes next. The first names a category: "If you host your apps on Replit and enable 'autofix' it becomes Self-Maintaining Software." The second extends the market to the public sector: "Introducing Replit for Government." The third addresses the risk side: "We wrote a whitepaper on how to properly sandbox agents to maximize productivity and reduce risk." Self-maintaining software is a different sales pitch from app generation, because it shifts the buyer's question from "can we build this" to "who fixes it at 3am," and autofix is an answer to that. Government as a segment is the harder half of the same argument: procurement, audit and security review are exactly the functions that a self-maintaining claim has to survive, which is presumably why the sandboxing whitepaper landed in the same window.

**中文：** Masad 今天三个动作，加在一起是对“应用生成往哪走”的一个完整论点。第一个动作给一个品类命名：“如果你把应用托管在 Replit 上并开启 autofix，它就变成了自维护软件。”第二个动作把市场推到公共部门：“推出 Replit for Government。”第三个动作处理风险面：“我们写了一份白皮书，讲如何正确地给 agent 做沙箱，以最大化生产力、降低风险。”自维护软件和“应用生成”是完全不同的销售话术，因为它把买家的问题从“我们能不能建出来”换成“凌晨三点谁来修”，而 autofix 就是这个问题的答案。政府作为客群是同一论证里更难的那半：采购、审计、安全审查，恰恰是“自维护”这个宣称必须活下来的关卡，沙箱白皮书在同一个窗口落地，大概不是巧合。

### 4. 前 Anthropic Claude Code 成员 Thariq — “会花很多钱、会让所有人失业”不是 AGI 立场

**EN:** Thariq pushed back on both ends of the doomer consensus in one pass: "basically everyone is bad at predicting how more intelligence will actually show up in the economy and the simplest answer of 'it'll cost a lot and unemploy everyone,' is actually not very AGI-pilled imo, we should avoid hyperstitioning it into reality." **中文：** Thariq 一次把末日论的两头都推了回去：“基本上所有人都很不会预测更多智能到底会以什么形式出现在经济里，而最简单的那个答案——‘会很贵，会让所有人失业’——我觉得其实一点都不 AGI-pilled，我们应该避免把它 hyperstition 成现实。”他给出的建设性版本传播得更广：“对经济末日论的解法，是更加 AGI-pilled。我相信一个对齐良好、能解释自己在做什么并且带着我们一起走的 ASI，一个让每个个体效能最大化的系统。”他抓住的词是 hyperstition，一个靠被反复讲述而自我实现的叙事；他瞄准的具体失效模式是：一份大规模失业的预测，逐渐变成一个招聘计划、一种监管姿态、一种自我实现的文化预期。他给出的对立立场有一样末日版没有的东西，就是可检验性：这个系统有没有让每个个体更有效，以及它能不能解释自己做了什么。

### No Priors — 《Taking on ServiceNow: Coding Agents for the Rest of Us with Serval Co-Founder and CEO Jake Stauch》

**The Takeaway：** 企业里 agent 的瓶颈不是智力，是权限——所以真正值钱的不是那个什么都能干的 agent，而是一套能让它“被批准去干”的流程。

**EN:** Serval was founded two and a half years ago by Jake Stauch and his co-founder Alex, and it was built to do something almost nobody attempts: replace ServiceNow inside large enterprises, not sit on top of it. The company went from 20 people in January to 200 today, and the ambition was stated before there was a product. Stauch's account of the design space is the part worth keeping.

The core insight is about who builds inside a company. Most of AI's enterprise adoption so far has been engineering teams, and not just because engineers are early adopters — it's because they have code review. A 19-year-old intern can build something powerful and the review process catches it before production. The rest of the company has no equivalent, so Serval takes the coding-agent primitives and wraps them in staging, approval cycles, permission checks and an audit trail, and hands that to IT. IT then extends it to HR, finance, legal. "For most of the world, they don't have an engineering department. They have an IT department."

Why personal agents stall in the enterprise is the sharpest observation in the episode. Your own permission set is so limited that even if you were allowed to bring your agent to work, "it can barely even read your emails." So the useful agent isn't the one with the most autonomy, it's the one that can borrow admin-level, service-account capability and then pay it back through deterministic approvals and just-in-time access. Stauch is explicit that this is "a little bit more annoying than the magical personal agent experience," and equally explicit that it's the only version a regulated enterprise will ever buy.

On the enterprise version of the moonshot, background agents that go find your problems and fix them, he's skeptical in a specific way: "On the one hand, you want these to be very autonomous. On the other hand, autonomy is very scary in the enterprise." Serval's answer is incremental escalation. The agent asks for capabilities one deterministic workflow at a time, you approve them, and over time its toolkit grows large enough to solve most problems. It's imperfect and annoying to babysit, and the alternative is an agent that can theoretically write code that acts on every system in the company, which is "an unbounded security risk that I don't think anyone's gonna be able to take anytime soon."

He also gives the most credible deflation of the SaaSpocalypse thesis to date, from someone who would benefit from it. Companies that vibe-coded their own systems of record rediscovered that enterprise software is expensive to build and to maintain, that edge cases need people, and that nobody wants to staff the internal tools team. Point solutions and middleware will consolidate into the core platforms; the systems of record survive and get replaced by a new generation of incumbents. The pricing conclusion follows: Serval charges per workflow run rather than per token, because "tokens is more like verbosity and inefficiency." And on hiring, the least intuitive claim of the hour: "because every employee is so much more productive, we actually want more of them, not less of them." The company has no product function at all. Engineers sit in customer Slack channels, most on a customer call a day, and make the product decisions at the edge.

**中文：** Serval 由 Jake Stauch 和联合创始人 Alex 在两年半前创立，做的是一件几乎没人敢碰的事：在大型企业里直接替换 ServiceNow，而不是在它上面加一层。公司从今年 1 月的 20 人涨到现在的 200 人，而那份野心在还没有产品的时候就宣布过了。Stauch 对这个设计空间的拆解，是这期里最值得留下的部分。

核心洞察关于“公司里谁在建设”。到目前为止，AI 在企业里的采用几乎全在工程团队，这不只是因为工程师是早期采用者，而是因为他们有 code review。一个 19 岁的实习生可以做出很强的东西，评审流程会在它上生产前拦住。公司其他部分没有等价物，所以 Serval 把 coding agent 的原语包进预发布、审批周期、权限校验和审计留痕，交给 IT；IT 再把它带到 HR、财务、法务。“对世界上大多数公司来说，他们没有工程部，他们有一个 IT 部。”

个人 agent 为什么在企业里走不动，是这期最锋利的观察。你自己的权限集太小了，就算公司允许你带 agent 上班，“它连你的邮件都几乎读不了。”所以有用的 agent 不是最自主的那个，而是能借用管理员级、服务账户能力，再通过确定性审批和即时授权把这笔账还回去的那个。Stauch 说得很直白，这“比那个魔法般的个人 agent 体验要烦一点”，也同样直白地说，这是受监管企业唯一会买的那一版。

对企业版“终极登月”——那些自己去找问题、再去解决的 background agent——他的怀疑很具体：“一方面，你希望它们非常自主。另一方面，自主在企业里非常可怕。”Serval 的答案是能力逐步升级：agent 一次申请一个确定性工作流的权限，你批准，时间长了它的工具箱足够大，能解决大部分问题。这不完美，盯着它很烦；而备选方案是一个理论上能写出作用于公司所有系统的代码的 agent，那是“一个我认为近期没人敢承担的无边界安全风险”。

他还给出了迄今对 SaaS 末日论最可信的一次降温，而且来自一个本该从中受益的人。那些自己 vibe coding 重写核心系统的公司重新发现：企业软件建起来贵、维护起来也贵，边角案例需要人，而内部工具团队这个岗位没人愿意去。点解决方案和中间件会向核心平台收敛；系统级记录会活下来，被新一代在位者替换。定价结论随之而来：Serval 按工作流运行次数收费，而不是按 token，因为“token 更像是啰嗦和低效”。至于招聘，是这一小时里最反直觉的一句：“因为每个员工都高效了太多，我们其实想要更多的人，而不是更少。”这家公司没有产品部门。工程师坐在客户的 Slack 频道里，大多数人每天至少接一个客户电话，产品决策在边缘上做。
