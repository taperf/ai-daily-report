### 2. Box CEO Aaron Levie

**EN:** Levie laid out why cybersecurity is about to be transformed by agents: "We'll likely have 1,000X more tokens flowing through cyber agents soon than we do today." As the surface area of cyber risk explodes, keeping up manually is already nearly impossible for most companies. He predicts agent swarms that fan out, leverage any security tool, test attack permutations, read logs, and process enterprise data for risk levels — a capability that only emerged in the past few months, once long-running agents, computer use, and cyber-trained models matured. He calls it a huge opportunity for every layer of the security stack, and at least one reason to be optimistic about the balance of offensive and defensive capability.

**中文：** Levie 论述了网络安全即将被 agent 改造的逻辑：“很快，流经网络安全的 agent 的 token 量可能是今天的 1000 倍。”随着网络风险面急剧扩大，人工防御早已跟不上（大公司都很勉强，绝大多数公司更不可能）。他预测很快会出现 agent 集群：自动调用各种安全工具、测试各种攻击变体、读日志、评估企业数据风险。这个能力是过去几个月才出现的——前提是 long-running agent、computer use 足够成熟、模型接受了深度网络安全训练。他认为这是安全栈每一层（身份、防火墙、网络、代码扫描、数据保护）的巨大机会，也是对攻防平衡保持乐观的理由。

### 3. Cursor 设计者 Ryo Lu

**EN:** Ryo Lu (who designed Cursor, Notion, Stripe) published a long, reflective essay titled "struggle together." His core worry: with AI, "we learn how to get results without necessarily understanding how it works. And because the result looks complete, it becomes easy to miss the questions we never learned to ask." He wonders what happens "when we remove the difficulty before we understand what it was teaching us." The counterweight he offers is human collaboration: working with people is uncomfortable, but "sometimes, after struggling together, you become a band" — you learn who notices what you miss, when to step in, when to let someone else carry the rhythm.

**中文：** 设计过 Cursor、Notion、Stripe 的 Ryo Lu 发了一篇长文《struggle together》（一起挣扎）。他的核心忧虑是：有了 AI，“我们学会了拿到结果，却不一定理解它是怎么运作的。因为结果看起来是完整的，我们很容易错过那些从没学会去问的问题。”他担心的是“在我们还没理解困难在教我们什么之前，就把困难移除了”。而他给出的平衡解是人与人协作：和人来一起工作并不舒服，各有诉求、各有历史、有人会推翻你，“但有时一起挣扎过后，你们会成为一个乐队”——你知道谁会注意到你遗漏的东西、什么时候该上、什么时候该让别人带节奏。

### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck: "Jev: The AI That Makes Decisions, Not Text — and Why It Changes Everything"（嘉宾 Diogo Almeida）

**The Takeaway:** LLM 的问题是不可靠也不可校准，让 AI 从“生成文本”转向“做决策”的机器原生智能，才是自动化真正跑通的前提。

**EN:** Jev founder Diogo Almeida, previously at OpenAI, argues that today's LLMs are "95% Einstein, 5% Mr. Bean — and if it doesn't tell you when it's the Mr. Bean, how are you gonna automate anything?" His critique is blunt: pretraining is "a giant dumpster fire of wasting resources," and models remain "incredibly non-composable" — they play nicely with nothing except other AI. Jev's answer is machine-native intelligence: instead of emitting text, systems composed of reliable, measurable classifiers where every decision branch can be scored, audited, and added to an eval when it errs — the same architecture, he notes, that made Google work. On the common objection that copying Jev is easy: "The people I think that were just completely copied by everyone in three weeks — and therefore there's nothing left… my guess is that they are dumb and non-robust." And on the perennial fear of an AI winter: "The AI winter I'm worried about is AI continuing to be so unbelievably useless at automation. And I just think that's not gonna happen anymore." He expects Jev's paradox to play out like semiconductors: as reliable automation finds stable use cases, demand explodes rather than contracts.

**中文：** Jev 创始人 Diogo Almeida（曾在 OpenAI 工作）认为，今天的 LLM 是“95% 爱因斯坦，5% 憨豆先生——如果它不告诉你什么时候是憨豆，你怎么可能自动化任何东西？”他的批评很直接：预训练是“浪费资源的巨大垃圾堆”，而模型“极不可组合”——除了和别的 AI 之外跟谁都玩不到一起。Jev 的答案是机器原生智能：系统不输出文本，而是由可靠、可度量的 classifier 组成，每个决策分支都可以打分、审计，出错就加进 eval——他同时指出，让 Google 成功运转的正是这套架构。对于“Jev 三周内就会被抄遍”的说法，他回应：“我的猜测是那些抄袭品又笨又不稳健。不过如果它们真能解决别人的问题，我乐见其成。”至于经久不衰的 AI 寒冬论：“我担心的 AI 寒冬，是 AI 在自动化这件事上继续无比没用——而我认为这不会再发生了。”他预期 Jev 会像半导体一样触发 Jevons 悖论：可靠的自动化找到稳定用例后，需求只会爆炸式增长，而非萎缩。
