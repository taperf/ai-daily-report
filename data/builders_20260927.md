### No Priors：77 亿美元"再创始"实验——买下一家老牌公司，用 AI 重做一遍｜Sequence Holdings 联合创始人兼 CEO Michael Lee

**The Takeaway [EN]:** The biggest AI winners of the next decade may not be startups attacking incumbents, but incumbents that get "re-founded" around AI — and Michael Lee just scaled that playbook to $7.7B by taking insurance brokerage Baldwin private with the Dell family office.

**核心结论 [中文]:** 未来十年 AI 最大的赢家，可能不是颠覆在位者的创业公司，而是围绕 AI 完成"再创始"（re-founding）的在位企业——Michael Lee 刚刚把这套打法放大到 77 亿美元：与戴尔家族办公室联手，将保险经纪公司 Baldwin 私有化，这是迄今规模最大的 AI 相关私有化交易。

**[EN remix]** Lee, a former Lone Pine and Apollo investor, started Sequence Holdings twenty months ago on a contrarian premise: some industries — insurance brokerage, banking — are structurally unassailable by startups (90% gross retention, carriers-not-customers pay the broker, underwriting-driven asset gathering), so instead of fighting the incumbent, buy the right one and forge the market leader with frontier engineering. Why not just hire consultants or buy software? Services firms optimize for "getting in your wallet, staying in your wallet, growing your wallet" — a path to incrementalism — and software is forced to sell to workflows as they exist today, since "you can't sell a product around a new human assembly line that doesn't exist." Ownership is the only way to align incentives for radical redesign. The proof point is BankSouth, a Georgia bank where Sequence's system cut average consumer underwriting time by 94% and took commercial loans from 30 days end-to-end down to 11 — and when loan volumes doubled in Q2, the bank absorbed it all with a *smaller* underwriting team. His most memorable line on discipline: "We're trying to do one deal per year. That's it. Our job is to do one." And on why traditional PE can't copy this: fund economics reward deploying capital on three-year horizons and celebrate the investor, while AI transformation demands a culture where "the celebrated persona is the engineer."

**[中文 remix]** Lee 曾在 Lone Pine 和 Apollo 做投资，二十个月前创立 Sequence Holdings，出发点是一个反直觉的判断：有些行业——保险经纪、银行业——在结构上就是创业公司打不进去的（客户留存率 90%、付钱给经纪的是保险公司而非客户、承保驱动资产汇集），所以与其挑战在位者，不如直接买下对的那一家，配上前沿工程能力，把它锻造成市场领导者。那为什么不请咨询公司或直接买软件？服务商的激励是"进你的钱包、留在你的钱包、掏空你的钱包"——只会带来渐进式改良；而软件产品只能卖给今天已存在的工作流，"你没法围绕一条还不存在的人类流水线卖产品"。只有所有权才能让激励对齐、支撑彻底的重新设计。证据是乔治亚州的 BankSouth 银行：Sequence 的系统把消费贷平均承保时长砍掉 94%，商用贷端到端周期从 30 天压到 11 天——今年 Q2 贷款量翻倍时，银行用一个**更小**的承保团队全部接住了。他谈自律时说得最狠："我们一年只做一单。就这样。我们的工作就是做好这一单。"至于传统 PE 为什么抄不了：基金结构激励的是三年周期内部署资本、崇尚的是投资人文化，而 AI 转型需要的是"被膜拜的角色是工程师"的组织。
