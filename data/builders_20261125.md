### Replit CEO Amjad Masad：Agent 集群规模化定律 + 会见美国副总统

Two significant posts. First, Replit published new research on **scaling laws for agent swarms**: scaling agent count yields dramatic gains — Opus 5.5 went from 3.7x speedup with 1 agent to **18.9x with 32 agents** on NumPy in a fixed two-hour window — but unlike traditional scaling laws, more compute doesn't guarantee better results, and how agents organize matters: decentralized swarms scaled better than centralized coordination, and individual model intelligence is a poor predictor of collective intelligence (GPT-6.2 Astra was the strongest solo agent but showed negative scaling beyond 8 agents). They also open-sourced **Optimization-Bench**. Second, Masad met with VP JD Vance to discuss American AI leadership and open-weight AI, warning: "America could win the frontier AI race and still lose the global AI ecosystem" as US allies seek sovereign AI and increasingly look to Chinese open models. He also welcomed Jensen back at Nvidia as "one of the most important champions of American open-weight AI."

Amjad Masad 今天两条重磅内容。其一，Replit 发布 **agent 集群（swarm）规模化定律**研究：固定两小时窗口内，Opus 5.5 在 NumPy 任务上从单 agent 3.7 倍加速提升到 **32 个 agent 的 18.9 倍**；但与传统 scaling law 不同，堆算力不必然带来更好结果，组织方式才是关键——去中心化集群比中心化协调扩展性更好，且单个模型智能水平并不能预测集体智能（GPT-6.2 Astra 单体最强，超过 8 个 agent 后反而负向扩展）。同时开源了 **Optimization-Bench**。其二，他会见副总统 JD Vance 谈美国 AI 领导力，警告「美国可能赢得前沿 AI 竞赛，却输掉全球 AI 生态」——盟友要主权 AI，正越来越多转向中国开源模型；并欢迎 Jensen 回归 Nvidia。

### Box CEO Aaron Levie：Agent 正在重写服务业的规模方程

Levie's long-form take: services businesses were always capped because scaling required headcount, while customers demanded faster, cheaper, higher-quality work. "Agents entirely change the calculus — output is now decoupled from time input. A single engineer can have the output of dozens; a single designer can build your entire design system and brand." But the winners won't be pure-agent plays: you still need experts on the front end to guarantee outcomes. He expects this pattern across cybersecurity, advertising, legal, accounting, and IT transformation — "a great time if you're starting one of these businesses." Bonus observation: "2026 is just waking up each day to the magnitude of news that could only have happened once per decade before 2026."

Box CEO Aaron Levie 的长文观点：服务业的规模上限历来是人数——需求再大，能招到的人才有限，而客户永远要求更快、更便宜、更好。**Agent 彻底改写了这个方程：产出与投入时间脱钩，一个工程师可以有几十个工程师的产出，一个设计师能搭完整个设计系统和品牌**。但赢家不会是纯 agent 公司——前端仍需要真正的专家确保交付结果。他预计网络安全、广告营销、法律、会计、IT 咨询等传统人力密集行业都将出现这个模式，「现在是创办这类公司的好时机」。另有金句：「2026 年就是每天醒来见证放在 2026 年前要十年才发生一次的大新闻。」
